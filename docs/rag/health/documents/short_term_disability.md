# Short-Term Disability Income

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Disability
**Document ID:** health/short_term_disability
**Guide:** Health & Employee Benefits Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `short_term_disability`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Weekly income-replacement benefit, short elimination period, benefit period typically capped at 2 years. Coordinates with mandatory state SDI in CA/NY/NJ/RI/HI.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Short- and long-term disability income replacement.
- Earned-income based with occupation class within limits
- Elimination and benefit periods selected

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Elimination period not satisfied
- Medical evidence not supporting the disability
- Self-inflicted or excluded conditions
- Occupation outside the product's class

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing condition period (3/12 or 6/12)
- Self-inflicted injury; war; normal pregnancy where excluded

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Return-to-work benefit
- Waiver of Premium
- Hospital-confiner benefit

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Monthly benefit to 60–70% of income (taxable or non-taxable)
- Maximum benefit period to age 65 / SSNRA

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age, sex, occupation class, and income
- Elimination and benefit periods
- Rider selections

## 12. Underwriting focus

Verify the weekly benefit against documented income (most filings cap replacement at 60-70%); confirm SDI coordination in an SDI state.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Benefit periods beyond the standard
- Occupational classes requiring rating
- Overlapping coverage

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
