## 1. Product overview

Restricted-enrollment Medicare Advantage plan for applicants with a qualifying chronic condition (C-SNP) or dual Medicare/Medicaid eligibility (D-SNP).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Special Needs Plans for dual-eligible, chronic, or institutional members.
- Meets SNP category (dual-eligible, chronic condition, institutional)

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No qualifying chronic or special-needs condition
- Not dual-eligible where the SNP requires it
- Residency outside the plan service area
- No Medicare Part A and Part B entitlement

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)
- Proof of address (utility bill / lease / bank statement)
- Date-of-birth verification (birth certificate / passport / driver's license)
- Completed application / enrollment form

## 12. Underwriting focus

No health-status decline, but enrollment REQUIRES a documented qualifying chronic condition or dual-eligible status — the inverse of a knockout gate.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Members not meeting SNP eligibility
- Out-of-area members

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
