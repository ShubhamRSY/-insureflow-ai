## 1. Product overview

Individual/family major medical sold on the ACA exchange — Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum metal tier. Guaranteed issue; premium-tax-credit eligible.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** ACA Marketplace metal-tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum) and off-exchange major medical.
- US citizens or lawfully present residents
- Open enrollment or a qualifying special enrollment period
- Metal tier matched to household income and subsidy

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Not lawfully present (where a subsidy is claimed)
- Outside the open or special enrollment window
- Residency outside the plan's service area
- Non-compliant (non-ACA) plan characteristics

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- No pre-existing-condition exclusion (ACA guaranteed issue)
- Non-EHB services and out-of-network care except emergencies

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Pediatric dental/vision (essential benefit)
- Cost-sharing-reduction variant
- Premium tax-credit reconciliation
- Standardized plan option

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Out-of-pocket maximum per ACA; no annual or lifetime caps
- Deductibles by metal tier with embedded family deductibles

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age curve, family composition, tobacco loading
- Rating area (geography) and APTC subsidy
- Community rating within age bands

## 12. Underwriting focus

No health-status underwriting (ACA §2702). Verify household income for subsidy eligibility and metal-tier fit to expected utilization.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Subsidy or immigration documentation issues
- Special-enrollment-eligibility disputes
- Out-of-state residency

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
