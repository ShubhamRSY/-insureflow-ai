# HSA-Qualified HDHP

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Individual

**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Guaranteed-issue ACA plan with a deductible at or above the IRS HSA-qualifying minimum, enabling tax-advantaged HSA contributions.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** High-deductible health plans paired with an HSA.
- US citizens or lawfully present residents
- Open enrollment or a qualifying special enrollment period
- Metal tier matched to household income and subsidy

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Plan design that is not a qualified high-deductible health plan
- HSA-ineligibility conflicts (other coverage, Medicare enrollment)
- Deductible below the IRS minimum
- Out-of-pocket maximum above the IRS limit

## 12. Underwriting focus

No health-status underwriting. Confirm the chosen deductible actually meets the IRS minimum for HSA eligibility.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Applicant covered by a non-HDHP
- Disqualifying coverage for HSA

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
