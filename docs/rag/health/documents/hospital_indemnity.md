# Hospital Indemnity

## 1. Product overview

Fixed dollar-per-day-of-hospitalization cash payout — pays regardless of the base plan's deductible or coinsurance and independent of actual billed charges.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Fixed cash benefit for hospital admissions/stays.
- Insured maintains an underlying base health plan
- Deductible selected (for example a $100K aggregate)

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No hospitalization evidence for a claim
- Benefit exceeding the policy's daily maximum
- Pre-existing hospitalization
- Coverage not in force on admission
