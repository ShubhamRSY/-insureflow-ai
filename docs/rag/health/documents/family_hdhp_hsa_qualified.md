## 1. Product overview

Family-tier HSA-qualified HDHP — aggregate family deductible at or above the IRS family HSA-qualifying minimum.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Family high-deductible plan with a family HSA.
- Two or more household members
- ACA-compliant family enrollment

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Plan design that is not a qualified HDHP
- HSA-ineligible members included in the plan
- Deductible or out-of-pocket outside IRS limits
- First-dollar coverage that disqualifies the HSA

- No coverage until the family deductible is met
- HSA rules limit first-dollar benefits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

## 12. Underwriting focus

No health-status underwriting. Confirm the chosen family deductible meets the IRS family HSA minimum.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Members with disqualifying coverage
- Mixed HSA eligibility

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
