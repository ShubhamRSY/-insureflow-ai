## 1. Product overview

DOL-regulated ERISA multi-employer plan sponsored by a bona fide trade, professional, or industry association covering multiple small employers.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Group health offered through a bona fide association of employers/members.
- Bona fide association meeting DOL/ACA tests
- Minimum participation

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Association that is not a bona fide group
- Members outside the association's class
- No commonality of interest among members
- State where the arrangement is restricted (MEWA rules)

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Non-bona-fide association arrangements (MEWA concerns)
- No health-status underwriting within the group

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Dental/vision
- Stop-loss (self-funded)

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Association size and participation
- Demographics
- Region

## 12. Underwriting focus

Verify bona fide association status (common purpose beyond offering insurance) before treating this as a single risk pool.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Association formed primarily to sell insurance
- Low participation

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
