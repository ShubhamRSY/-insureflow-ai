## 1. Product overview

ACA small-group market (1-50 FTE employees) — guaranteed issue, community-rated, no group-level experience rating.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** ERISA large-group (fully insured or self-funded) and ACA small-group (community-rated).
- Bona fide employer groups meeting minimum participation
- Small group 1–50 (state variation); large group 51+

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Group below the minimum participation
- No employer contribution toward premium
- Group outside the eligible size or state requirement
- Non-qualifying association treated as a small group

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Self-funded: stop-loss terms define exclusions
- No health-status underwriting within a group

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Dental/vision
- Life/disability bundle
- Employee-assistance program
- Telehealth

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Aggregate and specific stop-loss attachment points
- ACA essential health benefits and out-of-pocket caps

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Community rating (small group) and experience rating (large)
- Participation, contribution, and workforce demographics
- SIC class loadings

## 12. Underwriting focus

Verify employer size against the state's small-group threshold; no medical underwriting on any enrolling employee.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Group below minimum participation
- Non-standard class definitions
- Late entrants from a prior carrier

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
