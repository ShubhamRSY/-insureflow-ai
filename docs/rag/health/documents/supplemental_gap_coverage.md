# Supplemental / Gap Health Coverage

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Top Up
**Document ID:** health/supplemental_gap_coverage
**Guide:** Health & Employee Benefits Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `supplemental_gap_coverage`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Sits above a high-deductible base plan and pays once its deductible is exhausted — Standard Gap (per-incident) or Super Gap (annual aggregate).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Super top-up / gap coverage above a high-deductible base plan (per-incident or annual aggregate deductible).
- Insured maintains an underlying base health plan
- Deductible selected (for example a $100K aggregate)

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No underlying primary coverage in force
- Benefits exceeding the primary's gaps
- Duplicate coverage for the same expense
- Non-compliant coordination-of-benefits design

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)
- Proof of address (utility bill / lease / bank statement)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Claims already paid by the base plan (indemnity coordination)
- Pre-existing waiting where applicable

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Maternity gap module
- Prescription gap benefit
- Telehealth

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Top-up deductible (annual aggregate) between base and top-up
- Sum insured per top-up

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Base-plan sum insured and deductible
- Age and family size

## 12. Underwriting focus

Confirm the base plan's deductible amount — the gap policy is priced and triggered relative to it.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Coordination-of-benefits disputes
- Claims exceeding the primary's gap
- Pre-existing conditions

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
