## 1. Product overview

Pure death/dismemberment schedule only — no accident-medical-expense or weekly-indemnity benefit. Cheaper and narrower than the combined AD&D / Accident Indemnity product.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Standalone Accidental Death & Dismemberment cover.
- Issue ages 18–70 (varies); no medical underwriting for AD&D
- Occupational class within appetite

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Injury from an excluded activity or occupation
- No medical evidence of accidental injury
- Pre-existing conditions
- Attained age outside the product limits

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Suicide/self-inflicted
- Disease/illness
- War; flight other than as a common-carrier passenger

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Accidental death & dismemberment
- Hospital-indemnity rider
- Fracture/dislocation benefit

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Capital sum insured and dismemberment schedule
- No weekly income (that is the AD&D/indemnity product)

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age and occupation class
- Capital sum
- Family vs single

## 12. Underwriting focus

Occupation class is the dominant rating factor — a hazardous occupation (Class IV) is referred for underwriter review.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Hazardous occupation class
- Capital sum far above income

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
