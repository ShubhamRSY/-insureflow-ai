# Permanent Partial Disability

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Disability

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `disability_ppd`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Scheduled-injury lump sum (loss of a limb, sight, or hearing) — no income proof required since the payout is fixed by the schedule, not earnings.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Benefit for Permanent Partial Disability (dismemberment/loss of use schedules).
- Earned-income based with occupation class within limits
- Elimination and benefit periods selected

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No permanent partial impairment per the schedule
- Impairment percentage not medically assessed
- Excluded causes
- Claim beyond the policy term

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)
- Proof of address (utility bill / lease / bank statement)

## 12. Underwriting focus

Match the disclosed injury type to the correct schedule percentage; do not invent a schedule entry that isn't in the filed manual.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any pre-existing impairment
- Hazardous occupation

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
