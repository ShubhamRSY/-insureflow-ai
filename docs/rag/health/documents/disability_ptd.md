# Permanent Total Disability

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Disability
**Document ID:** health/disability_ptd
**Guide:** Health & Employee Benefits Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `disability_ptd`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Lump-sum-or-extended-benefit payout triggered by a permanent and total inability to work — not a periodic income-replacement benefit like STD/LTD.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Lump-sum or income benefit on Permanent Total Disability.
- Earned-income based with occupation class within limits
- Elimination and benefit periods selected

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No evidence of total and permanent disability
- Ability to work in any occupation not ruled out
- Excluded conditions
- Survival or waiting period not satisfied

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing conditions
- Self-inflicted; war
- Partial disability (not covered)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Return-to-work incentive

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Defined total of payments or a lump sum
- Strict total-disability definition

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Occupation class and age
- Benefit amount and payment structure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Confirm the disability determination is permanent and total, not a recoverable condition better suited to STD/LTD.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Impaired health or hazardous occupation
- Aggressive total-disability definitions

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
