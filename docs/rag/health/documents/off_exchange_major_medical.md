# Off-Exchange Major Medical

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Individual
**Document ID:** health/off_exchange_major_medical

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `off_exchange_major_medical`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

ACA-compliant individual major medical sold directly by the carrier — not subsidy-eligible, but the same guaranteed-issue floor as a Marketplace plan.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** ACA-compliant major medical sold off-exchange (no premium tax credit).
- US citizens or lawfully present residents
- Open enrollment or a qualifying special enrollment period
- Metal tier matched to household income and subsidy

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Applicant outside the eligible age band
- Residency outside the filed states
- State where the product is not filed
- Requested benefit design outside the filed schedule

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)
- Proof of address (utility bill / lease / bank statement)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- No PTC subsidy; still no health-status underwriting
- Non-EHB and out-of-network care except emergencies

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Maternity add-on module
- Dental/vision riders
- Prescription-management benefit
- Telehealth

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- ACA out-of-pocket maximum; no annual/lifetime caps
- Metal-tier deductibles

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age curve, family composition, tobacco
- Rating area
- No APTC subsidy

## 12. Underwriting focus

No health-status underwriting. Confirm the applicant understands no subsidy applies off-exchange.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Household expecting APTC
- Multi-state coverage needs

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
