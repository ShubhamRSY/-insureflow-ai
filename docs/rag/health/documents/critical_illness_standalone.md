## 1. Product overview

Lump-sum indemnity payment on first diagnosis of a covered condition (cancer, heart attack, stroke, kidney failure). Supplemental — does not replace major medical.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Lump-sum on first diagnosis of covered conditions (cancer, cardiac, stroke, etc.) and disease-specific cover.
- Issue ages 18–65 on a health-questionnaire basis
- Covered-condition list matched to the insured's risk profile

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Pre-existing critical illness within the waiting period
- Diagnosis not matching the policy's defined conditions
- Survival period not satisfied
- Condition excluded by the policy

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing waiting period (90–180 days)
- First-30-day exclusion for selected conditions
- Self-inflicted injury and war

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Second-event benefit
- Waiver of Premium
- Return-of-premium option

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Benefit per condition ($25K–$250K), independent or shared
- 25% early-stage payout with a reduced multiple

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age, tobacco, and family history
- Benefit amount and number of covered conditions
- Occupation class

## 12. Underwriting focus

Medically underwritten (health questionnaire). Confirm the lump-sum benefit amount is documented.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Claims within the waiting period
- Pre-existing diagnosis near inception
- High-value claims above the auto-pay limit

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
