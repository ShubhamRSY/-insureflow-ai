# ACA Catastrophic Plan

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Individual
**Document ID:** health/catastrophic_plan
**Guide:** Health & Employee Benefits Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `catastrophic_plan`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Guaranteed-issue, high-deductible ACA plan restricted to applicants under 30 or with a documented hardship/affordability exemption.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** ACA catastrophic plans for eligible individuals (under 30 or hardship exemption).
- Under age 30 or with a hardship exemption

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Applicant under 30 without a hardship exemption
- Non-qualified catastrophic plan design
- Enrollment outside the open or special window
- Income above the subsidy threshold where a subsidy is claimed

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)
- Proof of address (utility bill / lease / bank statement)

- Completed application / enrollment form

## 12. Underwriting focus

No health-status underwriting. Verify age-under-30 or hardship exemption documentation before issue.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Applicant over 30 without an exemption
- Needs pre-deductible coverage

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
