# Critical Illness Rider

**Effective:** 2026-01-01

**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Attaches to an existing base policy rather than standing alone — no independent free-look period or policy fee; terminates if the base policy lapses.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Critical-illness acceleration attached to a life or health base policy.
- Issue ages 18–65 on a health-questionnaire basis
- Covered-condition list matched to the insured's risk profile

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No base policy in force
- Rider not available at the attained age
- Condition onset before the rider effective date
- Rider sum outside the base-policy limits

## 12. Underwriting focus

Confirm the base policy exists and is in force before underwriting the rider.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Rider above the base policy limits
- Multiple riders stacking CI exposure

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
