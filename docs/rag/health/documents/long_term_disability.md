# Long-Term Disability Income

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Disability
**Document ID:** health/long_term_disability
**Guide:** Health & Employee Benefits Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `long_term_disability`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Monthly income-replacement benefit, longer elimination period, benefit period runs to a fixed term or to age 65.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Long-term disability income with a longer elimination period.
- Earned-income based with occupation class within limits
- Elimination and benefit periods selected

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No qualifying total disability
- Pre-existing conditions within the look-back
- Occupation-based exclusions
- Attained age outside the product limits

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing condition period
- Self-inflicted; war
- Normal pregnancy where excluded

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Own-occupation definition (2-year)
- Residual/partial
- COLA
- Future increase option

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Monthly benefit to 60% of income (integrated)
- Benefit period to SSNRA

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age, income, and occupation class
- Elimination and benefit period
- Rider selections

## 12. Underwriting focus

Benefit is capped as a percentage of pre-disability income — verify income documentation before issuing above the replacement ceiling.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Benefit above 60% of income
- Own-occ for high-risk occupations
- High-income financial underwriting

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
