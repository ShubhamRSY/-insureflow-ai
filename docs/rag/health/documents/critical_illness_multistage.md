# Multi-Stage Critical Illness

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Critical Illness
**Document ID:** health/critical_illness_multistage

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `critical_illness_multistage`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Pays a graduated percentage of the benefit at increasing severity stages instead of one lump sum on first diagnosis.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Multi-stage critical illness paying at early and advanced stages.
- Issue ages 18–65 on a health-questionnaire basis
- Covered-condition list matched to the insured's risk profile

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Stage outside the defined severity bands
- No progressive-stage medical evidence
- Waiting period not satisfied
- Duplicate claim for the same event

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing waiting period
- Self-inflicted; war
- Conditions diagnosed in the first 90–180 days

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Second-event benefit
- Angioplasty/early-stage benefit
- Return-of-premium

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Early-stage multiple (e.g., 25%) then advanced stage adds a further multiple
- Aggregate cap

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age/tobacco and family history
- Benefit amount and number of stages
- Occupation

## 12. Underwriting focus

Medically underwritten. Detailed baseline medical and family history required.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- History of a covered condition
- Multiple prior claims

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
