# Short-Term Limited Duration Insurance

## 1. Product overview

Not ACA-compliant — the one genuinely medically-underwritten individual health product in the US market. Banned or restricted in several states.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Short-duration, non-ACA major medical for coverage gaps.
- Health-questionnaire underwriting
- Duration within state limits (often < 3–12 months)

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Medical condition requiring ongoing treatment
- Requested duration beyond the state maximum
- Pregnancy or expected maternity
- Requested in a state that bans short-term limited-duration plans

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing conditions excluded (not ACA-compliant)
- No EHB guarantee; limited benefits
- Maternity and mental health often excluded

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Accident add-on
- Fixed hospital-indemnity rider
- Telehealth

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Fixed benefit maximum and duration
- No guaranteed renewability

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Health questionnaire and age
- State duration rules
- Benefit design

## 12. Underwriting focus

Full medical underwriting — pre-existing conditions can be permanently excluded. Confirm the issue state permits STLDI at all.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any pre-existing condition
- Applicant needing ACA-compliant coverage

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
