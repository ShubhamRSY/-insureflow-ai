## 1. Product overview

A single policy covering the applicant and dependents under one aggregate family deductible — the standard structure for a US family major-medical plan.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** A single policy covering the household under one aggregate family deductible.
- Two or more household members
- ACA-compliant family enrollment

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Dependents without proof of relationship
- Members resident outside the service area
- Late enrollment without a qualifying event
- Non-compliant plan design

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)
- Proof of address (utility bill / lease / bank statement)
- Date-of-birth verification (birth certificate / passport / driver's license)
- Completed application / enrollment form

## 12. Underwriting focus

Confirm dependent relationships and covered-lives count; no health-status underwriting on any covered member.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Late enrollees without a qualifying event
- Non-standard dependent relationships
- Members requiring medical underwriting

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
