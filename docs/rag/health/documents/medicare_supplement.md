# Medicare Supplement (Medigap)

**Line of business:** Health Insurance › Senior

**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Standardized Plans A, G, and N — pays Original Medicare's cost-sharing gaps. Guaranteed issue for 6 months after Part B starts; medically underwritten outside that window.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Medicare Supplement (Medigap Plans A/G/N) and Medicare Advantage (MA/SNP).
- Age 65+ or under-65 disability Medicare-eligible
- Guaranteed-issue window (six months around Part B) where applicable

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Not enrolled in Medicare Part A and Part B
- Under 65 without a qualifying disability
- Residency outside the plan's service area
- Requested during a closed enrollment with no guaranteed-issue right

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license / passport / state ID)
- Social Security number (for exchange subsidy eligibility and carrier verification)
- Proof of address (utility bill / lease / bank statement)
- Date-of-birth verification (birth certificate / passport / driver's license)
- Completed application / enrollment form

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing waiting (up to six months) if not guaranteed-issue
- No coverage beyond Medicare-covered benefits (Medigap)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Foreign-travel emergency
- Skilled-nursing coinsurance
- High-deductible variant

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Medigap Plan-specific benefit table (A/G/N/F)
- MA maximum out-of-pocket (in-network or combined) per CMS

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Issue-age, attained-age, or community rating
- Household and smoker discounts
- County / rating area

## 12. Underwriting focus

Determine whether the applicant is inside or outside the federal open-enrollment window — this decides whether medical underwriting applies at all.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Enrollment outside guaranteed-issue windows
- Rated or declined in underwriting states
- Foreign-travel benefit requests

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACA (PHSA §2702/2704), NAIC model regs, CMS Medicare guidance, state DOI rate filing
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
