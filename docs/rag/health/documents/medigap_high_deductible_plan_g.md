# Medigap High-Deductible Plan G

## 1. Product overview

CMS-standardized Medigap variant — much lower premium in exchange for a high annual deductible (~$2,870) before the plan starts paying.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Medigap Plan G with a high deductible.
- Age 65+ or under-65 disability Medicare-eligible
- Guaranteed-issue window (six months around Part B) where applicable

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Plan not standardized in the state
- Deductible structure non-compliant with the standard plan
- Enrollment outside the guaranteed-issue window
- Not Medicare-eligible
