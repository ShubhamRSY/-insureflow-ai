## 1. Product overview

A Medicare-replacement plan administered by a private carrier under CMS capitation — always guaranteed issue during an eligible enrollment period.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Medicare Part C — privatized Medicare benefits (HMO/PPO).
- Enrolled in Medicare Part A and B
- Lives in the plan service area

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Not entitled to Medicare Part A and Part B
- End-stage renal disease (in most circumstances)
- Out-of-network provider dependence
- Residency outside the plan service area

## 12. Underwriting focus

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Dual-eligible (route to SNP)
- ESRD (limited plans)

- **Authority:** Major medical is guaranteed-issue. Supplemental lines carry medical/occupational authority to $100K benefit; above refers.
