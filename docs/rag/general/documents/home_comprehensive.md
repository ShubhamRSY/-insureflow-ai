## 1. Product overview

Building + contents — deed, valuation, inventory, tax, interior + exterior photos.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Residential structure and contents — structure-only, contents-only, and comprehensive (Bharat Griha Raksha-type).
- Owner-occupier or tenant per the cover type; clear title where the structure is insured
- Sum insured based on reconstruction cost

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Combined structure and contents beyond the sum insured
- Excluded perils (flood/earthquake) not endorsed
- Vacancy beyond the permitted period
- Unrepaired prior damage

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Property ownership proof | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Property valuation report | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| List + invoices of insured contents | Documents the value, age, and condition of the insured property/equipment. |
| Property tax receipt | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Photographs (interior + exterior) | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- War and nuclear perils; wear and tear; inherent defect
- Flood and earthquake unless selected as add-ons

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Flood/earthquake extension
- Valuable-articles schedule
- Rent-receipt cover
- Public-liability link
- Emergency accommodation

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = reconstruction cost (structure) + contents value
- 80% average clause on the structure to avoid underinsurance

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Construction type, age of building, zone, and sum insured
- Loss experience and add-on selection

## 11. Underwriting focus

Both ownership and contents schedule. Interior + exterior photos.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Coastal, wind, and flood zones
- High-value contents above the schedule
- Aged roofs or unrepaired damage

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
