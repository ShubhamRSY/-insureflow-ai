## 1. Product overview

Goods/passenger CV statutory TP — fitness, permit, PUC.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Commercial-vehicle statutory third-party liability only.
- Valid RC, driving license, and PUC certificate
- Vehicle within age and IDV norms
- Insured discloses the previous policy and any NCB

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Commercial vehicle outside the permitted class
- Overloading beyond the registered capacity
- Invalid permits or licences
- Unroadworthy commercial vehicle

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| RC of vehicle | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Fitness certificate | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Permit copy (national / state permit) | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| ID + address proof of owner | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Driving license of driver | Verifies licensing and the driving exposure/experience of the operator. |
| PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- No own-damage cover (commercial vehicle, TP only)
- Driving without a valid license; DUI
- Use outside India; war/nuclear

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Owner-driver PA cover

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Statutory third-party limits (commercial vehicle)
- Owner-driver PA per tariff

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- GVW/passenger class and zone
- Driver/crew records

## 11. Underwriting focus

Fitness + permit + PUC are mandatory. Do not rate as private car TP.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Expired fitness/RC/permit
- Overloading or prohibited use

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
