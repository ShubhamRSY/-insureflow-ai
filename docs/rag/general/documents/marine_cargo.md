## 1. Product overview

Goods in transit — invoice, B/L or AWB, packing list, IEC, GST. Not individual KYC-only.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Cargo (goods in transit) and hull (vessel) — domestic and import/export.
- Insurable interest in the goods or vessel; declared consignments
- ICC(A/B/C) basis selected for cargo

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Cargo without shipping documents
- Inadequate packing or stowage
- Sanctioned trade
- Recurring losses from the same cause

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Invoice of goods being shipped | Documents the value, age, and condition of the insured property/equipment. |
| Bill of lading / airway bill | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Packing list | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Importer / exporter code (IEC certificate) | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Letter of credit (if applicable) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Company registration / GST certificate | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Inherent vice and ordinary leakage/breakage (ICC C)
- Insufficiency or poor packing unless D/B/U is insured
- War and strikes unless included

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Warehouse-to-warehouse extension
- War & strikes cover
- General-average contribution
- Debt-protection

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = CIF/invoice value plus 10%
- Hull per agreed value with Institute warranties

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Commodity type, packing, and route
- Carrier/conveyance type and ICC basis
- Annual turnover or declared volume

## 11. Underwriting focus

Invoice + bill of lading/AWB + packing list. IEC + GST for exporter/importer.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Single transits above $5M
- High-theft or hazardous commodities
- Sanctioned trade routes

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
