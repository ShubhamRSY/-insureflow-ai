## 1. Product overview

Overseas trip — passport mandatory, visa + itinerary.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Domestic and international travel — medical, baggage, and cancellation.
- Valid passport/visa and itinerary (international)
- Age within the policy band

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Travel to sanctioned or high-risk destinations
- Travel against medical advice
- No valid visa or travel documents
- Activities excluded by the plan (extreme sports)

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Passport (mandatory) | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Visa copy | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Travel itinerary / flight ticket | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| ID + address proof (home country) | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Age proof | Confirms the insured's age for rating and eligibility. |
| Photograph | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| International Travel | Passport (mandatory); Visa copy; Travel itinerary / flight ticket |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing conditions unless declared
- Travel against medical advice; hazardous sports unless added
- War and sanctions; pregnancy beyond the stated week

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Adventure-sports extension
- Annual multi-trip option
- Rental-car damage excess
- Flight-delay cover
- Political-evacuation/natural-disaster cover

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Medical sum insured ($50K–$1M international)
- Baggage and delay sub-limits
- Age-based plan bands

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Age, plan, trip duration/destination, and deductible
- Family/multi-trip vs single-trip

## 11. Underwriting focus

Passport knockout if missing. Cover follows itinerary dates.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Travel to sanctioned or high-risk countries
- Pre-existing conditions within the look-back
- High-value trip-cost plans

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
