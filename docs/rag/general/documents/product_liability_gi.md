## 1. Product overview

Product liability — catalog, manufacturing license, ISO/BIS, recall history.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Manufacturers/importers exposing product-related injury/damage liability.
- Professionals qualified and registered
- Products with quality controls and regulatory approvals
- Adequate turnover/headcount declaration

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Products with known defects or recalls
- Products sold into sanctioned markets
- Unlabelled or uncertified products
- Products with prior product-liability claims

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Company registration / GST certificate | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Product details / catalog | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Manufacturing license | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Quality certification (ISO / BIS, if applicable) | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Past claims / product recall history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Product Liability | Manufacturing license; Product details / catalog |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Recall costs unless covered
- Contractual liability beyond tort
- Fines and penalties

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Recall sub-limit
- Foreign territory
- Vendors endorsement

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Products aggregate and per-occurrence
- Recall sub-limit

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Product type, turnover, and exports
- Quality controls
- Loss history

## 11. Underwriting focus

Manufacturing license + product catalog. Recall history is a knockout/refer.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Export/US jurisdiction
- Any prior product claim or recall

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Company registration / GST certificate
- Product details / catalog
- Manufacturing license
- Quality certification (ISO / BIS, if applicable)
- Past claims / product recall history
