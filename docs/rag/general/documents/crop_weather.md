## 1. Product overview

Weather-index — land, KYC, bank, sowing, nearest weather station reference.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Producers using parametric weather-index cover (rainfall/temperature).
- Defined weather station and parameter near the plot
- Reliable historical weather data

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Weather-index cover without the required weather data
- Crop outside the covered region or index
- No insurable interest
- Backdated coverage

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Land ownership / tenancy proof | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Bank account details | Provides payout, premium-payment, and settlement instructions. |
| Sowing certificate | Confirms the notified crop/area or the parametric weather-index basis. |
| Nearest weather station reference (for indexed payout) | Confirms the notified crop/area or the parametric weather-index basis. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- No yield-based indemnity (parametric only)
- Areas without reliable station data
- War/nuclear

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Payout trigger levels
- Multiple-station indices

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Payout per trigger (parametric) × sum insured
- Sum insured per hectare

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Station selection and index design
- Trigger thresholds and payout rate
- Crop and season

## 11. Underwriting focus

Weather station reference is the index. Do not require yield history.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Pilot indices with thin data
- Large single-station concentration

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
