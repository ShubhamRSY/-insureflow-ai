## 1. Product overview

Public event — organizer GST, venue, local permit, artist contracts, ticket projection.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Wedding and concert/public event cancellation/delay and liability.
- Event date, venue, and attendance confirmed; permits in place
- Budget/revenue declared

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No public-authority permit or safety plan
- Crowd beyond venue capacity
- Key performer already in breach
- Adverse-weather exposure without a contingency plan

## 4. Submission requirements

| Venue booking agreement | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Weather unless an adverse-weather endorsement is purchased
- Change of mind; known circumstances at inception
- War and terrorism unless endorsed

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Adverse-weather cover
- Non-appearance extension
- Key-performer abandonment
- Cancellation/postponement

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = total budget/revenue at risk
- Cancellation/postponement cover; liability is separate

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Event type, duration, venue, attendance, and sum insured
- Peril endorsements and loss history

## 11. Underwriting focus

Event license/permit from local authority. Ticket projection for attendance risk.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Crowd above venue capacity
- Pyro/stunt/high-risk elements
- Key-performer non-appearance

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
