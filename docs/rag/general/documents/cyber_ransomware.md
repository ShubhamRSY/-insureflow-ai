# Cyber Insurance — Cyberattack / Ransomware Cover

## 1. Product overview

Ransomware / cyberattack — network details, existing controls, incident history.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Businesses needing cyberattack/ransomware first-party cover (BI + restoration).
- Completed security questionnaire with MFA, EDR, and backups in place
- Revenue and records volume declared

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No offline or immutable backups
- No endpoint-detection-and-response protection
- Prior ransomware event at inception
- Ransom payment that would breach OFAC sanctions

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Company registration / GST certificate | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| IT infrastructure & network details | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Existing cybersecurity measures declaration | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Past incident / breach history | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Cybersecurity audit report (if available) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Cyberattack / Ransomware | Existing cybersecurity measures declaration; IT infrastructure & network details |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- No privacy/third-party liability (that is the data-breach cover)
- Betterment; prior known incidents
- War/state-sponsored attacks

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Cyber extortion / ransom cover
- System restoration
- Business interruption
- Forensic/PR costs

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- First-party limit with extortion/restoration sub-limits
- Waiting period and retention

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Controls: MFA, offline backups, EDR, patch status
- Revenue and record count
- Prior incidents

## 11. Underwriting focus

Existing cybersecurity measures. Do not treat as data-breach-only wording.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- No offline backups or unsupported systems
- Any prior ransomware incident

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
