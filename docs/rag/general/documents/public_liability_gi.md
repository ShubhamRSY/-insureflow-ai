## 1. Product overview

Premises/public liability — GST, premises, nature of business, safety, claims.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Businesses and premises exposing third-party bodily injury/property damage.
- Professionals qualified and registered
- Products with quality controls and regulatory approvals
- Adequate turnover/headcount declaration

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Hazardous operations outside the accepted class
- Premises with unresolved safety hazards
- No public-access controls
- Premises with prior public-injury claims

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Company registration / GST certificate | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Business premises ownership / lease proof | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Nature of business declaration | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Safety compliance certificates (if applicable) | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Past claims history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Public Liability | Nature of business declaration; Business premises ownership / lease proof |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Injury to the insured's own employees (WC)
- Contractual liability beyond tort
- Fines and penalties

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Additional insured
- Waiver of subrogation
- Worldwide jurisdiction

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Indemnity plus defence (aggregate)
- Per-occurrence structure

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Occupancy/footfall and turnover
- Territory
- Loss history

## 11. Underwriting focus

Nature of business + premises. Safety certs if applicable.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- High-footfall/hazardous premises
- Any large public-liability claim

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Company registration / GST certificate
- Business premises ownership / lease proof
- Nature of business declaration
- Safety compliance certificates (if applicable)
- Past claims history
