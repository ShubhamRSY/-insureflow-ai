## 1. Product overview

Vessel hull — registration, class/seaworthiness, valuation, crew, owner company.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Vessel owners insuring the hull and machinery.
- Insurable interest in the goods or vessel; declared consignments
- ICC(A/B/C) basis selected for cargo

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Unseaworthy vessel
- No classification-society certificate
- Older hull without a current survey
- Owner without a maintenance program

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Ship / vessel registration certificate | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Vessel valuation report | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Classification society certificate (seaworthiness) | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Ownership proof of vessel | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Crew list & certification | Confirms the aircraft, crew, and aviation exposure. |
| Company registration documents (of ship owner) | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |

## 5. Applications & forms

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Hull & Machinery | Classification society certificate (seaworthiness); Crew list & certification |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Inherent vice; wear and tear
- War/strikes unless included
- Unseaworthiness

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- War & Strikes
- Increased value
- Loss of hire

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Agreed value of the vessel
- Institute warranties apply

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Vessel type, age, value, and flag
- Trade/route and class
- Loss history

## 11. Underwriting focus

Classification society certificate is seaworthiness. Do not rate as cargo.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- High-value hull
- War-zone or sanctioned trade

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Ship / vessel registration certificate
- Vessel valuation report
- Classification society certificate (seaworthiness)
- Ownership proof of vessel
- Crew list & certification
- Company registration documents (of ship owner)
