## 1. Product overview

Building only — ownership, valuation, tax, exterior photos.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Residential building structure-only cover for owners.
- Owner-occupier or tenant per the cover type; clear title where the structure is insured
- Sum insured based on reconstruction cost

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Dwelling unoccupied beyond the permitted period
- Unrepaired prior damage
- Construction outside the accepted classes
- Coastal or wind zones without the wind endorsement

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Property ownership proof (sale deed / registry) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Property valuation report / construction cost estimate | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Property tax receipt | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Photographs of property | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- No contents cover (structure only)
- War/nuclear; wear and tear; inherent defect
- Flood/EQ unless selected

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Terrorism
- Flood/EQ add-ons
- Alternative accommodation

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = reconstruction cost
- 80% average clause applies

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Construction, age, zone, sum insured
- Loss experience

## 11. Underwriting focus

Sale deed / registry required. No contents schedule on this leaf.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- High-value premises
- Coastal/EQ zones; heritage structures

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
