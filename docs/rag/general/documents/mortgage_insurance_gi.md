## 1. Product overview

Cover linked to a housing loan — sanction letter, property, income, loan statement.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Lender-linked cover on the mortgaged property/loan (fire and allied perils, sometimes housing-loan protection).
- Loan sanctioned and property mortgaged to the lender
- Sum insured aligned to the loan or reconstruction value

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No mortgage or loan documentation
- Loan already in default at inception
- Property outside the accepted region
- Borrower without insurable interest

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Loan sanction letter (from bank / lender) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Property documents (linked to the mortgage) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Income proof (salary slips / ITR) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Age proof | Confirms the insured's age for rating and eligibility. |
| Loan account statement | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- War and nuclear perils; wear and tear
- Wilful default or loss; tsunami/flood where not selected

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Disability-linked cover
- Unemployment extension
- Critical-illness rider

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = outstanding loan or reconstruction value
- Lender's interest per schedule

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Loan amount, term, and property value
- Zone and peril add-ons

## 11. Underwriting focus

Loan sanction + loan account. Distinct from the mortgage-lending vertical.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Loan in default or arrears
- Borrower with no insurable interest
- Non-standard property types

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
