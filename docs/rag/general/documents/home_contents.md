# Home Contents-only Cover

**Line of business:** General / Non-Life › Home
**Document ID:** general/home_contents
**Guide:** General Insurance Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `home_contents`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Movables only — inventory + invoices for high-value items. No deed required.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Residential contents-only cover for owners/tenants.
- Owner-occupier or tenant per the cover type; clear title where the structure is insured
- Sum insured based on reconstruction cost

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Contents without a valuation or inventory
- High-value items beyond the schedule
- Business or professional stock kept in the home
- Secondhand or undocumented items beyond limits

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| List of insured items with value (jewelry, electronics, furniture) | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Purchase invoices / bills of high-value items | Documents the value, age, and condition of the insured property/equipment. |
| Photographs of contents | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Contents Only | List of insured items with value (jewelry, electronics, furniture); Purchase invoices / bills of high-value items |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- No structure cover (contents only)
- War/nuclear; wear and tear
- Flood/EQ unless selected

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Specified valuable articles
- Flood/EQ add-ons
- Terrorism

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = contents value
- Sub-limits for valuables/electronics

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Contents value and category
- Zone
- Loss experience

## 11. Underwriting focus

Item schedule + invoices for jewelry/electronics. Do not require sale deed.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- High-value contents/valuables
- Coastal/EQ zones

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
