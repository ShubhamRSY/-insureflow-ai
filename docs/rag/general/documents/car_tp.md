## 1. Product overview

Statutory third-party motor — RC, owner KYC, DL. No own-damage inspection.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Private-car statutory third-party liability only.
- Valid RC, driving license, and PUC certificate
- Vehicle within age and IDV norms
- Insured discloses the previous policy and any NCB

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Unroadworthy or unsafe vehicle
- Invalid or unlicensed drivers
- Commercial use outside the private-car class
- Vehicle registered outside the state

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| RC (Registration Certificate) of vehicle | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| ID + address proof of owner | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Driving license | Verifies licensing and the driving exposure/experience of the operator. |
| Previous insurance policy copy (if renewal) | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Vehicle chassis / engine number details | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- No own-damage cover (private car, TP only)
- Driving without a valid license; DUI
- Use outside India; war/nuclear

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Personal accident cover for owner-driver (statutory)

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Statutory third-party (unlimited for death/disability; INR 15L property)
- Owner-driver PA per tariff

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Vehicle class/cc and zone
- Driver age/experience
- No IDV (no own damage)

## 11. Underwriting focus

RC + DL + owner KYC. Chassis/engine on RC. Prior policy only if renewal.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Expired RC/DL
- Commercial use under a private policy

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
