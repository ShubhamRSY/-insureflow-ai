## 1. Product overview

Companion pet — vaccination, medical history, breed/age, photo.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Companion-pet owners insuring veterinary and liability costs.
- Microchipped/identified pet with vaccination records
- Age within the plan band

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Pre-existing conditions
- Attained age outside the eligible band
- Animals used for breeding, fighting, or farming
- No veterinary records

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| ID + address proof of owner | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Pet's vaccination record | Verifies animal identity, health, and value. |
| Pet's medical history / health certificate | Supports medical underwriting, class assignment, and rating. |
| Breed & age proof (vet certificate / adoption papers) | Confirms the insured's age for rating and eligibility. |
| Photograph of pet | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Companion Pet | Pet's vaccination record; Breed & age proof (vet certificate / adoption papers) |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing/hereditary conditions
- Congenital conditions where excluded
- Cosmetic procedures

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Surgery/illness cover
- Third-party liability
- Wellness add-on

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Annual vet-fee limit and per-illness caps
- Third-party liability sub-limit

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Species, breed, age, and location
- Vaccination/health status
- Plan tier

## 11. Underwriting focus

Vaccination record + breed/age. Do not require livestock tag/valuation.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Brachycephalic/high-risk breeds
- Any pre-existing condition

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- ID + address proof of owner
- Pet's vaccination record
- Pet's medical history / health certificate
- Breed & age proof (vet certificate / adoption papers)
- Photograph of pet
