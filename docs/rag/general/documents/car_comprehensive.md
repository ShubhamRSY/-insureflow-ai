## 1. Product overview

Own-damage + TP. NCB needs prior policy; lapse/pre-owned needs inspection; new vehicle needs invoice.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Private cars and two-wheelers — statutory third-party and comprehensive own-damage.
- Valid RC, driving license, and PUC certificate
- Vehicle within age and IDV norms
- Insured discloses the previous policy and any NCB

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Pre-existing damage not disclosed
- Vehicle beyond the insured's insurable interest
- Heavily modified or racing vehicles
- Vehicles with a prior total-loss record

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| RC of vehicle | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Driving license | Verifies licensing and the driving exposure/experience of the operator. |
| Previous policy copy (for no-claim bonus transfer) | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Vehicle inspection report / photos (for pre-owned / renewal after lapse) | Supports medical underwriting, class assignment, and rating. |
| Invoice copy (for new vehicle) | Documents the value, age, and condition of the insured property/equipment. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Driving without a valid license; DUI
- Wear and tear; mechanical or electrical breakdown
- Use outside India; war and nuclear perils; contractual liability

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Zero-depreciation (Nil-dep) add-on
- Engine and gearbox protection
- Roadside assistance
- Consumables cover
- Towing & storage

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- IDV on age-based depreciation (5–50%)
- Third-party limits statutory (unlimited for death/disability; INR 15L property)
- NCB 20–50% of the own-damage premium

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Vehicle class, age, and engine capacity; IDV and zone
- Driver age/experience and voluntary deductible
- Add-on selections

## 11. Underwriting focus

NCB only with prior policy. Inspection if pre-owned or lapsed. Invoice if new.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Vehicles above the IDV threshold
- Heavily modified vehicles
- Vehicles with a total-loss history

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- RC of vehicle
- ID + address proof
- Driving license

- Vehicle inspection report / photos (for pre-owned / renewal after lapse)
- Invoice copy (for new vehicle)
