## 1. Product overview

SFSP on a dwelling — ownership, valuation, photos.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Residential standard fire and allied perils (SFSP).
- Clear occupancy, construction, and risk class
- Sum insured on a reinstatement-value basis

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Unoccupied or abandoned dwelling
- Unrepaired prior damage
- Unremediated electrical or wiring hazards
- Flammable storage in the home

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Property ownership proof | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Property valuation report | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Photographs of property | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Residential SFSP | Property ownership proof; Property valuation report |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- War/nuclear; wear and tear
- Own faulty design; neglect
- Flood/EQ unless allied perils selected

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Terrorism
- Earthquake/flood
- Removal of debris

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured on reinstatement value
- 80% average clause

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Construction, occupancy, zone
- Loss history
- LTA discount

## 11. Underwriting focus

Residential fire, not industrial occupancy. Ownership + valuation.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- High-value residences
- Coastal/EQ zones

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Property ownership proof
- ID + address proof
- Property valuation report
- Photographs of property
