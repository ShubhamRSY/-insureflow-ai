## 1. Product overview

Cattle/livestock — vet health cert, tag/photo/microchip, ownership, valuation.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Livestock (cattle) and companion pet insurance.
- Animal identification (ear-tag/microchip) and health certificate
- Cattle used for farming/dairy; pet with vaccination records

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Animals without health or vaccination certification
- Herd in a disease-control zone
- No ownership or valuation records
- Mortality beyond the covered causes

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| ID + address proof of owner | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Animal health certificate (from veterinarian) | Verifies animal identity, health, and value. |
| Animal identification (tag number / photo / microchip if applicable) | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Ownership proof of animal (purchase receipt) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Valuation certificate (by vet or authorized valuer) | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Photographs of animal | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Death from pregnancy complications unless covered; theft without a police report
- Pre-existing/congenital conditions (pet); intentional slaughter

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Mortality extension
- Theft cover
- Transit cover
- Veterinary-fee benefit

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = market value (cattle) / breed value (pet)
- Vet-fee limits and per-illness caps (pet)

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Species, breed, age, location, and use
- Health/vaccination status; number covered

## 11. Underwriting focus

Animal ID + vet health + valuation. Not pet companion wording.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Herd value above the delegated limit
- Disease-quarantine zones
- Theft-cover requests in high-crime areas

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
