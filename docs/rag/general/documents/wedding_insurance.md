## 1. Product overview

Wedding cancellation/liability — date/venue/budget, vendor contracts, advances.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Couples/planners insuring wedding cancellation and liability.
- Event date, venue, and attendance confirmed; permits in place
- Budget/revenue declared

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Event without a signed venue or vendor contract
- No defined cancellation or postponement dates
- Pre-existing cancellation reason
- Vendor no-show treated as covered without endorsement

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| ID + address proof of proposer | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Event details (date, venue, budget breakdown) | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Vendor contracts (venue, caterer, decorator) | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Advance payment receipts (for cancellation cover) | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Wedding Event | Event details (date, venue, budget breakdown); Vendor contracts (venue, caterer, decorator) |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Weather unless endorsed
- Change of mind; known circumstances
- War/terrorism unless endorsed

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Adverse weather
- Vendor non-appearance
- Public liability

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Cancellation up to the wedding budget
- Liability separate

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Budget, venue, and date
- Lead time and guest count
- Peril endorsements

## 11. Underwriting focus

Event date + vendor contracts + advance receipts for cancellation cover.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Outdoor/peak-season weddings
- Short lead time

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
