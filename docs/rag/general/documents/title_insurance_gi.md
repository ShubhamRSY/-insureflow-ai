## 1. Product overview

Legal title risk — deed chain, encumbrance, tax, survey, title search. Not motor/health KYC pattern.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Legal title chain, encumbrance, and title-search insurance.
- Title search/abstract showing marketable title
- Registered documents with a clear chain and encumbrance statement

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Known title defects or tax liens
- Chain-of-title gaps
- Fraudulent or forged deeds
- Jurisdiction where the product is not filed

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Property sale deed / title deed | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Property ownership chain documents (past 12–30 years records) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |

| Property tax receipts | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Survey / site plan of property | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Known defects and encumbrances at inception
- Government acquisitions/reservations if disclosed
- Illegal construction or unauthorized layout

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Survey cover
- Encumbrance/defect endorsement
- Builder/developer endorsement

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = property value or loan amount
- Cumulative / property-value cap

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Property value, loan amount, and locality
- Title-search depth and cleared encumbrances

## 11. Underwriting focus

Encumbrance certificate + title search report are core. Ownership chain 12–30 years.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Known title defects or liens
- Chain-of-title gaps
- Survey or encroachment issues

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
