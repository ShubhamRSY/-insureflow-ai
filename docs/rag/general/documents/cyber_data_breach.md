## 1. Product overview

First/third-party data breach — security policy, data volume, incident history.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Data breach (privacy liability and response) and cyberattack/ransomware (business interruption and restoration).
- Completed security questionnaire with MFA, EDR, and backups in place
- Revenue and records volume declared

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No security controls (multi-factor authentication, backups)
- Prior breach not remediated
- Storing data without a lawful basis
- Inability to accept the nation-state/APT exclusion

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Company registration / GST certificate | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| IT infrastructure details / data security policy | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Details of data handled (customer / employee data volume) | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Past cyber incident history (if any) | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Cybersecurity audit report (if available) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Data Breach | IT infrastructure details / data security policy; Details of data handled (customer / employee data volume) |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Betterment; prior known incidents
- War and state-sponsored attacks (market-dependent)
- Non-cyber business interruption; unencrypted portable-device losses

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Business-interruption extension
- Regulatory-defence cover
- PCI fines & penalties
- Restoration/data-recovery cover

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Separate liability and first-party limits with sub-limits
- Cyber-extortion / funds-transfer-fraud sub-limits
- Retention tiers by revenue

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Revenue, record count, industry, and controls maturity
- Prior incidents; retention and sub-limits

## 11. Underwriting focus

Data volume + security policy. Audit optional; incident history drives refer.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Any prior breach or ransomware event
- Data volumes above the threshold
- Nation-state/APT attribution

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Company registration / GST certificate
- IT infrastructure details / data security policy
- Details of data handled (customer / employee data volume)
- Past cyber incident history (if any)
- Cybersecurity audit report (if available)
