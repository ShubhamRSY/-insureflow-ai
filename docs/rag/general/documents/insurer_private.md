## 1. Product overview

Private-market KYC — income proof for higher SI; digital e-KYC via Aadhaar/PAN.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Purchase-KYC for private-insurer products (identity/address norms).
- Retail: KYC per provider type (PSU/GIC vs private insurers)
- Reinsurance: ceding insurer's registration, portfolio, and loss data

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- KYC mismatch or unverifiable identity
- No valid proof of identity/address for a private-insurer product
- Application against a withdrawn private-insurer tariff

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Age proof | Confirms the insured's age for rating and eligibility. |
| Photograph | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Income proof (for higher sum insured products) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Digital KYC (e-KYC via Aadhaar / PAN) | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Non-standard provider KYC
- Risk outside the private insurer's filed tariff
- Any proposal requiring private-insurer underwriting flexibility

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
