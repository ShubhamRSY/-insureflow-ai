## 1. Product overview

Not individual KYC. Ceding license, treaty/fac, portfolio, loss history, solvency, regulator clearance.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Provider-type purchase KYC (PSU vs private) and B2B reinsurance (insurer buying cover).
- Retail: KYC per provider type (PSU/GIC vs private insurers)
- Reinsurance: ceding insurer's registration, portfolio, and loss data

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Unlicensed ceding insurer
- New ceding relationship without loss data
- Cross-border layer without regulatory/AML clearance

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Ceding company's (primary insurer's) registration & license details | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Treaty / facultative reinsurance agreement | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Risk portfolio details of ceding insurer (policies being reinsured) | Documents the reinsurance structure and the ceding insurer's profile. |
| Loss history / claims data of ceding insurer | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Solvency & financial statements of ceding insurer | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Regulatory approval documents (IRDAI or relevant regulator clearance) | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Treaty terms and exclusions govern the reinsurance
- Retail exclusions per the underlying product

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Treaty/facultative terms
- Portfolio withdrawal and cession rights
- Profit-share and sliding-scale commission

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Retention, cession percentages, and aggregate ceilings (reinsurance)
- PI sum insured per treaty layer

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Ceding company's loss experience and portfolio profile
- Treaty structure, commission, and profit-share

## 11. Underwriting focus

Company-to-company. Decline if treated as retail KYC. Treaty + solvency + claims data.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Any cross-border reinsurance layer
- New ceding relationship without loss data
- Aggregate exposures above the treaty capacity

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
