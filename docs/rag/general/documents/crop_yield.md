## 1. Product overview

Yield/PMFBY-style — land record, Aadhaar, bank, sowing, acreage, crop-loan link.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Yield-based (PMFBY-type) and weather-index (parametric) crop cover.
- Notified crops and areas for the season; loanee and non-loanee
- Weather-index for a defined station and parameter

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Acreage reported after the acreage-reporting deadline
- Crops grown outside the approved practice
- No actual-production-history or yield records
- Land in a prohibited conservation use

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Land ownership / tenancy proof (khasra / khatauni / 7-12 extract) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| ID + address proof (Aadhaar mandatory in most schemes) | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Bank account details (for subsidy / payout credit) | Provides payout, premium-payment, and settlement instructions. |
| Sowing certificate (proof of crop sown) | Confirms the notified crop/area or the parametric weather-index basis. |
| Land area details (survey number, acreage) | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Loan account details (if farmer has crop loan, insurance often auto-linked) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Losses from war or nuclear perils
- Malicious damage by the insured
- Yield loss below the indemnity/deductible level

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Replanting benefit
- Yield-adjustment option
- Post-harvest cover

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured = scale of finance / loan value
- Index payout per trigger (parametric) or yield shortfall

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Crop, season, area, and sum insured; PMFBY premium subsidy
- Index parameters, trigger thresholds, and payout rate

## 11. Underwriting focus

Land + sowing + survey/acreage. Bank for subsidy/payout. Loan account if crop loan.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Acreage above the delegated threshold
- Non-standard crops or practices
- Claims with an acreage-reporting dispute

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
