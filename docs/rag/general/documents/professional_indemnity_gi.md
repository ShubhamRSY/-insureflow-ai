# Professional Indemnity Insurance

## 1. Product overview

PI for licensed professionals (medical / legal / CA).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Professional indemnity, public liability, and product liability (monoline liability).
- Professionals qualified and registered
- Products with quality controls and regulatory approvals
- Adequate turnover/headcount declaration

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Professionals with lapsed licensure
- Prior professional-negligence claims
- Firms outside the accepted professions
- Revenue not reconcilable to filings

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Professional license / registration certificate (e.g. medical / legal / CA license) | Supports medical underwriting, class assignment, and rating. |
| ID + address proof | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| Business / practice registration proof | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Details of services offered | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Past claims history (if any) | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed application/proposal form for this product (plus any state-mandated disclosures).

## 6. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Professional Indemnity | Professional license / registration certificate (e.g. medical / legal / CA license); Details of services offered |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Fines and penalties; punitive damages
- Contractual liability assumed beyond tort
- Recall costs unless covered
- Loss of goodwill / economic loss unless endorsed

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Retroactive cover
- Defence-costs cover
- Sub-contractor cover

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Indemnity plus defence costs (aggregate), INR 25L–INR 5Cr
- Per-occurrence/aggregate structure with sub-limits by peril

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Turnover, fee income, and profession/class
- Claims experience and deductibles
- Territory and jurisdiction

## 11. Underwriting focus

Professional license is mandatory. Services offered + claims history.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Professions outside the accepted list
- Retroactive-cover requests
- Prior claims or litigation disclosure

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Professional license / registration certificate (e.g. medical / legal / CA license)
- ID + address proof
- Business / practice registration proof
- Details of services offered
- Past claims history (if any)
