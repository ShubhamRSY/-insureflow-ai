## 1. Product overview

Factory/commercial fire — GST, asset valuation, fire safety cert, stock.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Residential (SFSP) and commercial/industrial fire with allied perils.
- Clear occupancy, construction, and risk class
- Sum insured on a reinstatement-value basis

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Unprotected (Class 10) construction
- Combustible or flammable storage without controls
- Non-compliant fire or safety systems
- Unrepaired prior fire damage

## 4. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Property / factory ownership or lease documents | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Company registration / GST certificate | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Asset valuation report (building + machinery + stock) | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Fire safety compliance certificate | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Stock / inventory statement | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Photographs of premises | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Commercial / Industrial Fire | Fire safety compliance certificate; Stock / inventory statement |

## 7. Policy exclusions

- War and nuclear perils; wear and tear
- Own pollution; faulty design; neglect

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Reinstatement of sum insured
- Removal-of-debris cover
- Loss-of-rent extension
- Business-interruption link

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Sum insured on reinstatement value; 80% average clause
- Debris-removal and architect-fee sub-limits

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Occupancy/risk class, construction, and protection (sprinklers)
- Loss history and long-term agreement discount

## 11. Underwriting focus

Fire safety compliance + stock/inventory. Occupancy is industrial/commercial.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Unprotected construction or high-hazard occupancy
- TIV above the authority limit
- Non-compliant fire systems

- **Authority:** Catalog products are underwritten manually. Accept within filed product terms; refer any non-standard term, add-on, or sum insured above the product limit.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, All India Fire Tariff, Motor Tariff, ICC (marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
