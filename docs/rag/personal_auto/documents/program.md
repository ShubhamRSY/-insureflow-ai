# Personal Auto Underwriting Program

**Line of business:** Personal Auto
**Document ID:** personal_auto/program
**Guide:** Personal Auto Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Program status:** filed

## 1. Program overview

Personal Auto program for standard eligible risks within filed program limits.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** US personal auto (PAP) for private-passenger vehicles.
- Valid license and MVR within program guidelines
- Garaged vehicle with permitted use
- At least state-minimum liability

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- DUI or two or more at-fault accidents in three years
- Commercial or livery use under a private policy

## 4. Submission requirements

| Requirement | Document types | Why it is required |
|---|---|---|
| Auto application | Auto Application; ACORD XML | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |
| MVR / driving record | MVR Report | Verifies licensing and the driving exposure/experience of the operator. |
| Vehicle declarations | Vehicle Declarations; Dec Page | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Claims / loss history | Loss Run; Home Claims History | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed program application (ACORD or carrier-specific) plus state-mandated disclosures.

## 6. Coverage schedule

_Coverages per the filed program forms (see Policy exclusions and Endorsements below)._

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Racing and intentional acts
- Delivery/rideshare use unless endorsed
- Regular availability of non-listed vehicles

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Rideshare (TNC) endorsement
- Comprehensive and collision
- Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist
- Roadside assistance and rental reimbursement

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- State-minimum liability and UM/UIM
- Comprehensive/collision with deductible tiers

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Age, gender, marital status, territory, and vehicle symbol
- MVR violations, prior insurance, and credit-based score where allowed

## 11. Underwriting focus

Confirm eligibility, condition, and rating factors for the risk per the Personal Auto program before binding.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- High-value or collectible vehicles
- Non-standard drivers and SR-22/FR filings

- **Authority:** Field authority to program limits; refer non-standard drivers, high-value vehicles, and SR-22/FR filings.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO Personal Auto Program (PAP), state financial-responsibility limits, NAIC
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Auto Application
- ACORD XML
- MVR Report
- Vehicle Declarations
- Dec Page
- Loss Run
- Home Claims History
