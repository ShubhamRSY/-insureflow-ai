## 1. Product overview

Kidnap, ransom, extortion, and related crisis-response coverage.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Companies and executives with personal security exposure (travel, high-profile roles).
- Operators holding class-specific certification and licensing
- Documented risk-management programs and contingency plans

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Travel to sanctioned or high-risk countries without a security plan
- Insureds with a history of abduction claims
- No reliance on a specialist security or crisis-response firm
- Requested coverage for non-permitted parties under the definitions

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| K&R / extortion application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Travel schedule to high-risk regions | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| List of insured persons / roles | Documents the benefit-plan design, participation, and funding structure. |
| Security and travel protocols | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Prior extortion / K&R incidents | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Corporate structure and ownership | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Response consultant preferences | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Prior K&R declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- War and government-ordered seizure
- Ransom or response expenses not incurred with the insurer's prior consent
- Bodily injury/property damage

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Crisis-response services
- Extortion/detention
- Evacuation and repatriation

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- $1M–$10M per occurrence typical
- Consultant and response-cost sub-limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Travel patterns and jurisdictions
- Industry and profile
- Security controls

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review travel patterns to high-risk countries, executive profile, and security protocols.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- High-risk jurisdictions
- Any prior incident or threat

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
