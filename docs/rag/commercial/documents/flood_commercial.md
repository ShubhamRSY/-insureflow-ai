## 1. Product overview

Commercial flood coverage — NFIP and/or private market excess flood.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Commercial property owners/tenants needing standalone flood cover (private or excess over NFIP).
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Property in a special flood hazard area rated without an elevation certificate
- Lowest-floor elevation below base flood elevation
- Prior repeated flood losses with no mitigation
- Unanchored or manufactured structures in the floodplain

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| FEMA Flood Zone determination | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| Elevation certificate | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| Prior flood loss history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Building and contents values | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Photos of lowest floor / utilities | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Lease or ownership proof | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Existing NFIP or private flood declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| ACORD / flood application | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- War and nuclear perils
- Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
- Earth movement (separate earthquake cover)
- Perils insured by the companion property policy

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Contents-only
- Excess Flood over NFIP
- Basement Contents
- Terrorism

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Building and contents limits per schedule
- Basement/contents sub-limits
- Maximum per-location program capacity
- 30-day waiting period for newly added coverage

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- FEMA flood zone and elevation certificate
- Occupancy and lowest-floor elevation
- NFIP vs private placement
- Community Rating System (CRS) credit

## 12. Underwriting focus

Confirm flood zone, elevation, prior flood losses, and contents vs. building values.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any building in a V-zone or below base flood elevation without mitigation
- TIV above program flood capacity

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
