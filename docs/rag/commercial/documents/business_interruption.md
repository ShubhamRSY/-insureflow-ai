# Business Interruption (Business Income) Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Property
**Document ID:** commercial/business_interruption
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `business_interruption`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Covers lost income and extra expense after a covered property loss.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Insureds whose income depends on the continued operation of an insured location or its supply chain.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No underlying property policy in force
- Business-income worksheet that cannot be reconciled to financial statements
- Dependence on a single supplier or customer with no contingency plan
- Start-up with no trading history to support projected income

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

| Document | Why it is required |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- No direct physical damage to the insured's own property (time-element cover only)
- Loss from utilities, denial of access, or a supplier/customer unless endorsed
- Loss of market, cash, and non-continuing expenses
- Ordinary payroll unless specifically insured

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Contingent Business Interruption
- Civil Authority / Denial of Access
- Extra Expense
- Extended Period of Indemnity
- Ordinary Payroll

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- 12-month indemnity period with a 72-hour waiting period
- Monthly limit = projected gross earnings plus continuing expenses
- Extended period of indemnity 30–90 days

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Realism of the business-income worksheet (revenue, continuing expenses, extra expense)
- Continuing-expenses and ordinary-payroll election
- Indemnity period vs realistic restoration time
- Dependent suppliers/customers and civil-authority exposure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Validate BI worksheet realism, dependent properties, indemnity period, and civil authority exposure.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Indemnity period above 12 months
- Single-customer dependency above 30% of revenue

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
