# Self-Insured Retention (SIR) / Fronting Arrangements

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Alternative

**Effective:** 2026-01-01

**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Large-deductible / SIR programs and fronted policies requiring collateral and claims administration.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Insureds using a large self-insured retention with admitted fronting paper for certificates and regulatory compliance.
- Demonstrated financial capacity for the retained layer
- Fronting carrier with A-rated admitted paper and collateral

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Fronting without a licensed fronting carrier
- Collateral or trust not funded to the self-insured-retention level
- Unrated or captive reinsurers without collateral
- Regulatory fronting limits exceeded

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Losses within the self-insured retention (retained by the insured)
- Risks outside the fronted coverage grant
- Punitive or exemplary damages where uninsurable

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Collateral/trust funding schedule
- Fronting-fee structure
- Claims-handling authority
- Reinsurance-recoverables endorsement

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- SIR $100K–$5M per occurrence, aggregate where specified
- Fronted admitted policy limits typically $1M–$5M
- Collateral equal to a multiple of the retained layer

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Size and funding of the self-insured retention
- Loss-pick assumptions and collateral (LOC/trust)
- Fronting fee (typically 5–10% of SG&A)

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review collateral agreements, claims administration contracts, loss forecasts, and fronting carrier terms.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- SIR above $1M without actuarial sign-off
- Insufficient collateral for the retained layer

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
