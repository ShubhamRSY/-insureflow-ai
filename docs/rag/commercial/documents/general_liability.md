# General Liability (CGL)

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Liability
**Document ID:** commercial/general_liability
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `general_liability`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Premises/operations, products & completed operations, and personal/advertising injury.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Contractors, manufacturers, distributors, service operations, and professional firms needing CGL and liability limits up to $2M/$4M.
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Bars, taverns, and late-night alcohol classes (write liquor liability)
- Asbestos or lead abatement contractors
- Ongoing pollution exposure without a pollution policy
- Unlicensed or unregulated operations

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| ACORD 126 (General Liability Section) | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |
| Description of operations | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Revenue by class of business | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Loss run reports (3–5 years) | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Subcontractor agreements & their Certificates of Insurance (COI) | Confirms subcontractor insurance and risk-transfer via certificates of insurance (COI). |
| Safety program documentation | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Prior GL policy declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Financial statements — last 2–3 years | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

## 6. Applications & forms

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Expected or intended injury
- Contractual liability other than insured contracts
- Absolute pollution exclusion unless amended
- Damage to your work and product recall
- Professional / E&O exposures (separate form)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Additional Insured (owners, lessors, contractors)
- Waiver of Subrogation
- Primary & Non-Contributory
- Designated Premises / Products-Completed Operations
- Hired & Non-Owned Auto

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- $1M / $2M general aggregate as the default
- Products-completed-operations aggregate (shared or separate)
- Fire legal liability $50K–$300K
- Damage to premises rented to you $100K
- Employee benefits and employers' liability where applicable

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Class codes with payroll, sales, or area exposures
- Experience modification where applicable
- Deductible or SIR election
- Territory and state catastrophe loading

## 12. Underwriting focus

Evaluate operations hazard class, contractual risk transfer, products exposure, and loss history.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Products-completed operations above $1M
- Hazardous classes (demolition, roofing above three storeys)
- Residential or HOA exposures

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
