# Group Disability Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Workforce
**Document ID:** commercial/group_disability
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `group_disability`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Short-term and long-term group disability income benefits.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Employers providing short- and long-term group disability income.
- State-governed workers' compensation for eligible employment classes
- Captive or self-insured groups for qualifying industries
- Documented safety program for high-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Groups without 12 months of payroll and class data
- High-turnover classes priced with no credible experience
- No statutory-disability integration established
- Reduced-hours or partial-disability classes without a plan design

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Group disability application (STD/LTD) | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Census with occupations and salaries | Documents the benefit-plan design, participation, and funding structure. |
| Plan design (elimination, benefit %, max) | Documents the benefit-plan design, participation, and funding structure. |
| Prior STD/LTD claims experience | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Return-to-work / absence management program | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Participation rates | Documents the benefit-plan design, participation, and funding structure. |
| Prior disability declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Financial statements | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

- Group disability application

## 7. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Short-Term Disability (STD) | STD plan design (elimination period, benefit duration); Prior STD claims experience |
| Long-Term Disability (LTD) | LTD plan design (elimination, benefit %, max); Prior LTD claims duration and offsets |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pre-existing condition period
- Self-inflicted injury; war

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Own-occupation (2-year) for select classes
- COLA
- Return-to-work incentive

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Monthly benefit to 60% of income (integrated with other income)
- Maximum benefit period to SSNRA

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Industry and job class
- Participation and integration offset
- Prior claims

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review occupation mix, elimination periods, benefit %, and prior LTD claim duration.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- High-income/high-benefit populations
- Adverse disability claims experience

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
