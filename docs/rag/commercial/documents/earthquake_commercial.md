## 1. Product overview

Earthquake shake and related earth-movement coverage for commercial risks.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Commercial property with earthquake exposure, standalone or as a DIC layer.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Masonry or non-ductile concrete with no seismic retrofit
- High-seismic-zone risk without a probable-maximum-loss study
- Probable maximum loss above appetite
- Contents-only request with no building resilience data

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Seismic zone/fault line proximity report | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| Building retrofit/reinforcement documentation | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| Structural engineering report | Confirms the project scope, schedule, and construction risk. |
| SOV with construction and soil details | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Prior quake / earth-movement loss history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Building and contents values | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Financial statements | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Prior earthquake declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- War and nuclear perils
- Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
- Flood (separate cover)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Sprinkler Leakage — Earthquake
- Excess of primary EQ
- Business income extension

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Percentage deductibles 2–5% of TIV
- Per-location and aggregate limits
- Sprinkler-leakage sub-limit

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Seismic zone and site/soil conditions
- Construction type and building age
- Retrofit credits (bracing, anchorage)
- Loss history

## 12. Underwriting focus

Assess seismic zone, construction/retrofit, soil, and deductible/attachment structure.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Unreinforced masonry in a high seismic zone
- Single-schedule TIV above $50M

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
