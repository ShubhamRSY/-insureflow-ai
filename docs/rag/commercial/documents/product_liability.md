# Product Liability Insurance

## 1. Product overview

Liability arising from manufactured, distributed, or sold products.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Manufacturers, distributors, and importers with products-completed-operations exposure.
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Products with known defect litigation history
- Pharmaceutical or medical devices without a recall-history review
- Products sold into sanctioned markets
- Dietary or cosmetic products with unsubstantiated claims

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Product specifications and safety testing reports | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Manufacturing/quality control documentation | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Product liability claims history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Certificates of compliance (UL, CE, etc.) | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Products liability application / supplemental | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Product list with sales by SKU / category | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Warning labels / IFU / SDS samples | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Prior product liability declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Contractual liability assumed beyond tort
- Recall and withdrawal expense
- Damage to the insured's own product
- Impaired property and unsold stock

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Limited Recall
- Vendors endorsement
- Foreign products territory

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Products-completed operations aggregate
- Recall/withdrawal sub-limit
- Per-occurrence limit

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Product type and annual receipts
- Distribution geography and foreign exposure
- Quality-control and testing program
- Loss history

## 12. Underwriting focus

Scrutinize product type, recalls, warnings/labels, distribution geography, and claims severity.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Foreign (US/Canada) distribution
- Any prior recall or product lawsuit

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
