## 1. Product overview

Covers the cost of rebuilding to current code after a loss (endorsement or standalone).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Owners of older buildings where code upgrades trigger post-loss reconstruction costs.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Building that is not code-compliant with the local jurisdiction
- Requested limit exceeds the available building coverage limit
- No valuation of demolition and undamaged-portion costs
- Post-loss reconstruction that triggers a zoning non-conformity

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Contamination and pollution cleanup
- Demolition not required by code
- Loss to the undamaged portion unless endorsed

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Undamaged portion of the building
- Demolition Cost
- BI period-of-restoration coordination

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Increased Cost of Construction 5–25% of the limit
- Demolition cost sub-limit
- Combined ICC + demolition cap

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Building age and code adoption date
- Jurisdiction code enforcement
- Total insured value

## 12. Underwriting focus

Compare building age and construction to current code; quantify undamaged portion, demolition, and increased cost of construction.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Pre-code buildings in strict jurisdictions
- ICC limits above 25% of TIV

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
