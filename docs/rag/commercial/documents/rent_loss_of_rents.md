# Rent / Loss of Rents Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Property
**Document ID:** commercial/rent_loss_of_rents

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `rent_loss_of_rents`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Standalone business-income style cover for landlords — lost rental income after a covered loss.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Landlords and owners exposing rental income to a covered physical loss.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No lease or rent-roll documentation
- Vacancy or rent roll that cannot be verified
- Rent loss requested beyond the property indemnity period
- Tenant-default risk treated as covered

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Loss where there is no physical damage to the premises
- Lease cancellation or non-renewal
- Rent-control or regulatory changes
- Consequential loss

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Extended Period of Indemnity
- Civil Authority / Denial of Access
- Lease cancellation sub-limit

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Monthly gross rent × indemnity period
- 12-month default indemnity
- Fair rental value basis

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Rental value and occupancy
- Tenant concentration and lease terms
- Indemnity period

## 12. Underwriting focus

Validate rent roll quality, lease terms, vacancy, and indemnity period realism.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Single-tenant dependency above 50% of income
- Indemnity period above 18 months

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
