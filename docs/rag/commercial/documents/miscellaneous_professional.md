# Miscellaneous Professional Liability (MPL)

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Liability
**Document ID:** commercial/miscellaneous_professional
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `miscellaneous_professional`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Catch-all professional liability for professions not otherwise classified (consultants, HR firms, etc.).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Unclassified professional-service classes not written on a dedicated E&O form.
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Contractors providing medical or legal advice without a licensed principal
- Home-inspection firms offering structural certifications
- Any class with known prior professional liability claims
- Revenue from prohibited referral-fee arrangements

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| MPL application with detailed services description | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Sample client contracts / engagement letters | Reviews contractual obligations, indemnities, and risk-transfer terms. |
| Revenue breakdown by service type | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Professional licenses / certifications (if any) | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Quality control / risk management procedures | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Loss runs — professional liability | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Marketing materials / website service claims | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Prior MPL / E&O declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

- Intentional acts
- Fee refunds
- Bodily injury/property damage

- Territory

## 12. Underwriting focus

Define services precisely, review engagement letters, and assess claim patterns for the specific profession.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Classes resembling healthcare or construction design
- Any known circumstance

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
