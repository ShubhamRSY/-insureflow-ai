## 1. Product overview

Hull, cargo, and P&I for ocean and coastal marine exposures.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Cargo in international transit and ocean-going hull/vessel exposures.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Vessels trading to sanctioned ports (OFAC/ITAR)
- Hull older than 25 years without a current survey
- Bulk or liquid cargo without a formal stowage and stability plan
- Trades outside the agreed trading warranty

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Vessel documentation (registration, survey report) | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Voyage/route plans | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Cargo manifests | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Crew certification records | Confirms the aircraft, crew, and aviation exposure. |
| Marine application (hull / cargo / P&I) | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Loss runs and casualty history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| P&I club or prior marine policy history | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Prior ocean marine declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Inherent vice and ordinary leakage
- Insufficiency of packing unless D/B/U is insured
- War and strikes unless included
- Delay and loss of market

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- War & Strikes
- Duty and Brand (D/B/U)
- Annual Declaration / Sales Turnover
- Classification / ISO warranty

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Cargo: CIF/invoice value plus 10%
- Hull: agreed value subject to Institute warranties

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Commodity, packing, and route
- Conveyance type and ICC(A/B/C) basis
- Voyage vs annual declaration volume

## 12. Underwriting focus

Evaluate vessel class, trading limits, cargo types, crew experience, and P&I club arrangements.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Hull vessel value above $10M
- War-zone or sanctioned-trade voyages

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
