## 1. Product overview

IP infringement defense/abatement and related intellectual property covers.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Companies enforcing or defending IP rights (patents, trademarks, copyrights).
- Operators holding class-specific certification and licensing
- Documented risk-management programs and contingency plans

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Patents with known invalidity or prior-art challenges
- Trademarks with pending oppositions
- IP held in a sanctioned jurisdiction
- No freedom-to-operate analysis

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |

| IP portfolio schedule (patents, marks, copyrights) | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Freedom-to-operate / clearance opinions (if any) | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Prior IP litigation / cease-and-desist history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |

| Competitor landscape notes | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Revenue tied to insured IP | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |

## 7. Coverage schedule

_Coverages per the filed product schedule._

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Known infringement/invalidity before inception
- Intentional infringement
- Fines and penalties

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Defense-cost-only
- Enforcement (abatement) cover
- Multi-jurisdiction

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Per-claim/aggregate with defense sub-limits
- Enforcement expense sub-limit

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Portfolio strength and jurisdictions
- Litigation history and freedom-to-operate
- Industry

## 12. Underwriting focus

Assess patent/trademark portfolio, freedom-to-operate, litigation history, and industry litigiousness.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any known dispute or opinion of counsel
- NPE/high-litigation sectors

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
