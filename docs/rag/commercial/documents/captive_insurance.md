# Captive Insurance Programs

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Alternative
**Document ID:** commercial/captive_insurance
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `captive_insurance`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Self-owned insurance subsidiaries writing parent/affiliate risks — requires actuarial and regulatory feasibility.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Mid-to-large organizations forming a captive insurer or protected cell to retain and finance their own risk.
- Captive feasibility study and actuarial funding in place
- Board-approved risk-retention policy and domicile selection

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Feeder or participant programs without a feasibility study
- Captive domicile without regulatory licensing
- Risks outside the captive's approved class
- No actuarial funding study

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Risks outside the captive's approved business plan
- Losses exceeding the captive's policy limits and aggregate
- Punitive or exemplary damages where uninsurable

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Excess-of-loss reinsurance
- Aggregate stop-loss
- Cash-flow / loss-funding plan
- Domicile regulatory-reporting endorsement

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Retention funded to the actuarial loss picks
- Fronted admitted limits of $1M–$5M over the captive
- Aggregate stop-loss attachment per the funding study

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Captive domicile and regulatory capital requirements
- Actuarial loss picks and funding adequacy
- Fronting fee and collateral structure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review actuarial feasibility study, captive business plan, domicile, capitalization, and fronting/reinsurance structure.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any new captive domicile to the program
- Funding below the actuarial central estimate

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
