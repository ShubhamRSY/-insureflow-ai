# Non-Trucking Liability ("Bobtail") Insurance

**Effective:** 2026-01-01

**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Liability for owner-operators driving without a load (bobtail / non-trucking).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Owner-operators under lease to a carrier for non-trucking (bobtail) use.
- Private-passenger-to-commercial (classes 3–6), light/medium trucks, and tractors
- Drivers with valid CDL/MVR within program thresholds
- Garaged in an eligible state

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- For-hire carriers operating outside the intrastate radius
- Truckers hauling hazardous or oversize loads without endorsements
- Owner-operators without a written lease
- Uninsured or underinsured subcontractors

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Non-trucking liability (bobtail) application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Lease agreements with motor carriers | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Owner-operator / independent contractor schedule | Verifies dependency, relationship, and the beneficiary arrangement. |
| Vehicle schedule (power units) | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Driver MVRs | Verifies licensing and the driving exposure/experience of the operator. |
| Loss runs — auto / bobtail | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Radius and deadhead/bobtail usage description | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Prior non-trucking / bobtail declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Any use under the carrier's authority (deadhead under dispatch)
- Owned-trailer liability unless endorsed
- Cargo

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Trailer interchange
- Non-owned trailer

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- CSL $1M
- Non-owned trailer sub-limit

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Radius and lease terms
- Unit type
- Driver MVR

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review lease agreements with motor carriers, deadhead/bobtail exposure, and primary vs. excess structure.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Ambiguity over when dispatch begins/ends
- Any recent collision

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
