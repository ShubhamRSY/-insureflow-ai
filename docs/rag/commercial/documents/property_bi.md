# Commercial Property Insurance

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `property`

## 1. Product overview

Building, contents, and related property coverages for commercial locations.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Commercial real estate owners and tenants, manufacturers, warehousers, retail, and hospitality seeking all-risk or named-peril property programs.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Unprotected frame (ISO Class 10) construction with no sprinkler or hydrant credit
- Heavy manufacturing with unrated combustible dust or flammable-liquid storage
- Vacant or unrepaired buildings carried beyond the vacancy provision
- Coastal wind or brush exposures without a companion wind/flood policy

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Statement of Values (SOV) — per-location address, construction, occupancy, protection, exposure (COPE) | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| ACORD 140 (Property Section) | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |
| Property appraisal / replacement cost valuation | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Building specs: year built, construction type, roof age/type, square footage | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Fire protection report: sprinklers, alarms, hydrant distance, fire dept. response time | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Photos of exterior, roof, electrical panel, and any hazard storage areas | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Loss run reports (3–5 years) | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Lease agreement (tenant) or title deed (owner) | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Flood zone certificate / elevation certificate | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| Financial statements (for BPP/contents valuation) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Flood and earthquake (written separately or via a DIC form)
- Wear, tear, deterioration, and inherent vice
- Mold to the extent of the sub-limit
- Employee dishonesty (crime form)
- Ordinance or law unless endorsed
- Utility services interruption unless endorsed

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Replacement Cost / Extended Replacement Cost
- Agreed Value
- Coinsurance waiver
- Blanket limits across scheduled locations
- Spoilage and Equipment Breakdown (where applicable)

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Building and BPP on a replacement-cost basis; coinsurance 80–100%
- Debris removal 25% of the loss plus $25K
- Mold $10K–$50K sub-limit
- Business income 12-month default extended period
- Spoilage $5K–$25K

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- COPE — construction, occupancy, protection, and exposure
- Rating territory and public protection class
- Age and condition of roof, wiring, plumbing, and HVAC
- Three-to-five-year loss history and loss ratio
- Deductible selection (AOP standard; separate wind/hail)

## 12. Underwriting focus

Evaluate construction, fire protection, location hazards (flood/earthquake), valuation adequacy, and COPE + SOV quality.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Named windstorm in coastal tier 1/2
- Aggregate TIV above $50M
- Locations with an unsatisfactory COPE survey

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
