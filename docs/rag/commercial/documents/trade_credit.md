## 1. Product overview

Protects receivables against buyer default — domestic and export exposures.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Financial and credit exposures — trade credit, surety bonds, political risk, representations & warranties, and legal expense.
- Acceptable obligor/buyer credit ratings
- Bonded principals with demonstrated capacity and collateral
- M&A transactions with vetted, audited financials

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Buyers without credit history or audited financials
- Obligors in sanctioned or embargoed countries
- Single-buyer concentration above appetite
- Transactions without documentary shipping evidence

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Fraud or concealment by the insured
- Known pre-existing breach (R&W)
- War and confiscation unless endorsed (political risk)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Whole-turnover endorsement
- Specific-account endorsement
- Discretionary credit-limit increase
- Extended-terms cover

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Discretionary credit limit per buyer
- Bond penalty up to 100% of contract value
- Aggregate indemnity cap (R&W typically 10% of deal value)

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Buyer/obligor credit rating
- Industry sector and country risk
- Transaction tenor, deductible, and retention
- Book loss experience

## 12. Underwriting focus

Analyze buyer creditworthiness, customer concentration, AR aging, credit policy, and historical bad-debt experience.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Single-buyer limits above $500K
- Emerging-market obligors
- Policies exceeding 90-day credit terms

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
