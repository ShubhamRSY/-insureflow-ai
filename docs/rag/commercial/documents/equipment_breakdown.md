# Equipment Breakdown (Boiler & Machinery) Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Property
**Document ID:** commercial/equipment_breakdown
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `equipment_breakdown`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Sudden and accidental breakdown of boilers, machinery, and critical equipment.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Owners of boilers, pressure vessels, mechanical/electrical equipment, and HVAC systems.
- Equipment with a current inspection/boiler certificate where required

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Equipment with no maintenance records or manufacturer schedule
- Boilers or pressure vessels without a current inspection certificate
- Assets beyond economic repair (obsolete, parts unavailable)
- High-voltage switchgear without thermographic surveys

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Equipment inspection/maintenance records | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Age and service history of covered equipment | Documents the value, age, and condition of the insured property/equipment. |
| Manufacturer specs and warranty documents | Documents the value, age, and condition of the insured property/equipment. |
| Prior EB loss runs | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Equipment schedule with values | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Financial statements | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Prior policy declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Equipment breakdown application | Confirms equipment condition, age, and inspection/maintenance history. |

## 7. Coverage schedule

_Coverages per the filed product schedule._

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Normal wear, tear, corrosion, and gradual deterioration
- Obsolescence and rebuilding to code unless endorsed
- Loss from water/process not attributable to an equipment breakdown
- Terrorism unless endorsed

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Spoilage
- Utility Services / Service Interruption
- Data Restoration
- Hazardous Substances cleanup

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Per-item equipment sub-limits
- Spoilage 10–25% of the limit
- Extra expense and business income for 12 months
- Hazardous-substances cleanup sub-limit

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age, condition, and type of equipment
- Boiler/pressure-vessel inspection history
- Spoilage and utility-service exposure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review equipment age/maintenance, inspection certificates, and business interruption from outage.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Aged pressure vessels without inspection
- Spoilage values above $1M

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
