# Business Owner's Policy (BOP)

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Package
**Document ID:** commercial/bop
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `bop`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Bundles property + general liability for eligible small businesses.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Small commercial and mid-market accounts bundling property and liability under one BOP or CPP.
- Eligible occupancy combined with property and liability cover
- Building and BPP values within program limits
- Revenue and payroll within BOP/CPP thresholds

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Classes outside the eligible BOP list (bars, contractors above threshold)
- Buildings with habitational or heavy-manufacturing uses
- Revenue or building value above the BOP caps
- Unprotected coastal or wind exposures

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| BOP application (ACORD 125/126/140 as required) | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |
| Location schedule with COPE details | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Description of operations | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Gross sales / payroll | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Loss runs — property and liability | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Prior BOP / package declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Lease or ownership proof | Confirms insurable interest, ownership, and the lender's interest. |
| Financial statements (if requested) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Property and CGL exclusions apply line by line
- Professional liability
- Auto and workers' compensation (separate forms required)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Business Income / Extra Expense
- Equipment Breakdown
- Employee Dishonesty
- Hired & Non-Owned Auto
- Additional Insured endorsement

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Building and BPP per the BOP/CPP program schedule
- CGL $1M/$2M with 12-month business income
- $10K/$25K deductibles typical

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Loss cost × exposure by coverage
- Package credit and multi-coverage discount
- Territory and protection class
- Deductible selection

## 12. Underwriting focus

Confirm BOP eligibility (size/class), property COPE, and GL operations fit.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any class outside the BOP eligibility list
- Revenue above the BOP threshold
- Properties in coastal or wildfire tiers

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
