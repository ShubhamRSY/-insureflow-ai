## 1. Product overview

Aircraft hull and liability for owned, leased, or operated aircraft.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Owners/operators of aircraft — hull and liability, private and commercial.
- Current airworthiness certificate and crew certification
- Pilot experience within open-pilot requirements

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Operators without a current airworthiness certificate
- Crew without type ratings or valid medical certification
- Operations in sanctioned airspace
- Fleets with open NTSB or incident investigations

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Aviation hull & liability application | Confirms the goods/vessel, route, and transit documentation. |
| Aircraft schedule (N-number, make, year, value) | Confirms the aircraft, crew, and aviation exposure. |
| Pilot experience forms (hours, ratings) | Confirms the aircraft, crew, and aviation exposure. |
| Use description and geographic limits | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Hangar / airport details | Confirms the aircraft, crew, and aviation exposure. |
| Maintenance program | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Loss / incident history | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Prior aviation declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- War/hijacking unless endorsed
- Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
- Use outside the declared purpose

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Non-owned aircraft
- Premises liability
- Search-and-rescue
- Hangarkeepers

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Hull on agreed value
- Liability CSL per occurrence
- Passenger sub-limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Aircraft make/model/value
- Crew experience and hours
- Use (private, charter, instruction)
- Territory

## 12. Underwriting focus

Evaluate aircraft type, pilot experience/hours, hangar, and use (private vs. commercial).

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any operator without current certification
- Commercial charter use
- Hull value above $10M

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
