## 1. Product overview

First-party recall costs and related product contamination covers.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Food, consumer-goods, and auto-parts manufacturers with recall/withdrawal exposure.
- Operators holding class-specific certification and licensing
- Documented risk-management programs and contingency plans

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Products with known contamination at inception
- Undeclared allergens or regulatory non-compliance
- Products already subject to a recall order
- Unapproved overseas manufacturing

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Product recall application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Product list and sales by category | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Traceability / lot-tracking procedures | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Prior recalls and FDA/USDA actions | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Crisis / recall response plan | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |

## 7. Coverage schedule

_Coverages per the filed product schedule._

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Fines and penalties
- Intentional/criminal acts
- Loss of market share unless endorsed
- Non-accidental contamination

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- First-party recall costs
- Third-party recall liability
- Rehabilitation expense
- Consultant costs

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- $5M typical with a 12-month discovery period
- Recall/consultant sub-limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Product type and distribution
- Quality/food-safety controls
- Recall history

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review product type, recall history, traceability, and crisis-communication readiness.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any prior recall
- Products with catastrophic (botulism/allergen) exposure

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
