## 1. Product overview

Professional liability for services and advice — profession-specific applications.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Professional-service firms and consultants selling advice or services for a fee.
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Professionals with lapsed licensure or discipline actions
- Firms with a pattern of large claims or pending litigation
- Revenue that cannot be reconciled to tax filings
- New ventures with no operating track record

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Description of services offered | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Sample client contracts/engagement letters | Reviews contractual obligations, indemnities, and risk-transfer terms. |
| Professional licenses/certifications | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Loss run reports | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Quality control/risk management procedures | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| E&O application (ACORD 126 / carrier) | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |
| Prior E&O policy declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Revenue breakdown by service line | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |

## 6. Applications & forms

- ACORD 126 or carrier E&O application (profession-specific)

## 7. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Medical Malpractice | Medical license and board certification; Hospital privileges documentation; Patient volume/procedure mix data; NPDB report |
| Legal Malpractice | Bar admission/license verification; Practice area breakdown; Conflict-check procedures document |
| Technology E&O | Service/product description (SaaS, consulting, etc.); Client contract templates with liability caps; Data handling/security policy |
| Real Estate E&O | Real estate license; Transaction volume/value history; Errors/complaint history |
| Insurance Agents/Brokers E&O | E&O license and appointment letters; Book of business summary; Carrier contracts |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Intentional/dishonest acts
- Fines and penalties
- Fee refunds
- Bodily injury/property damage unless endorsed
- Prior acts before the retroactive date

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Extended Reporting Period
- Retroactive date change
- Defence costs outside the limit
- Subcontracted professional services

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Claims-made per-claim/aggregate
- Defence inside the limit (or first-dollar)
- Extended reporting period 90–365 days

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Professional fee income and class
- Retroactive date and claims history
- Territory/jurisdiction
- Sub-limit and defence-cost structure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Scrutinize nature of services, past claims, contract quality, revenue mix by service line, and risk-management procedures.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- US/Canada jurisdiction exposure
- Any known circumstance or prior claim
- Fee income above $10M

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
