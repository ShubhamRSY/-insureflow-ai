# Workers' Compensation Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Workforce

**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `workers_comp`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Employee injury coverage with payroll class codes, e-mod, and safety programs.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Employers of all sizes for workers' compensation, employer's liability, and group workforce benefits.
- State-governed workers' compensation for eligible employment classes
- Captive or self-insured groups for qualifying industries
- Documented safety program for high-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Non-compliant or unlicensed operations
- Classes with unfunded prior workers' compensation liability (successor liability unresolved)
- Employee-leasing/PEO arrangements without a concurrent employee agreement
- Exposures not permissible in the applicable state plan

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

| Document | Why it is required |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Employment-practice penalties (written under EPLI)
- Domestic employees (separate policy)
- Federal employment classes (Longshore, FELA) unless extended

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Waiver of Subrogation
- Increased Employers Liability limits
- Alternate Employer
- Voluntary Compensation
- USL&H (where applicable)

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Statutory workers' compensation benefits
- Employer's liability $100K/$500K/$100K — raise to $1M/$1M/$1M
- Employer's liability policy aggregate

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Class rate per $100 of payroll
- Experience Modification Factor (EMR)
- Schedule and premium credits
- State assessments and surcharges

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review safety programs, injury history, industry hazard class, payroll by NCCI class, and experience modification.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Employers with unfunded prior compensation liability
- Experience modification above 1.30
- New operations in monopolistic states

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
