## 1. Product overview

Covers buildings and materials during construction or renovation.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Owners, general contractors, and developers insuring a project under construction or renovation.
- Defined project with a construction schedule and completed value
- Contractor with relevant experience and no unresolved defects claims

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Projects with no defined completion date, budget, or completed value
- Frame construction above four storeys without a fire plan
- Renovation over 50% of an occupied building with no hot-work permit process
- Existing structures included without a valued COPE survey

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Existing structures unless the project is a renovation
- Wear, tear, and faulty workmanship
- Testing/commissioning and performance failure unless endorsed
- Contractors' tools and equipment (separate cover)
- Soft costs and delay unless endorsed

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Soft Costs / Delay in Completion
- Design-Build Professional
- Expediting Expense
- Water Damage — testing
- Maintenance period extension

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Completed value of the project plus soft costs
- Soft costs 10–25% of hard costs
- Delay-in-completion 3–12 months
- Per-location and catastrophe sub-limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Project value, duration, and type (new vs renovation)
- Location CAT and flood-zone exposure
- Contractor experience and use of wraps
- Off-site storage and transit exposure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Assess construction type, project duration, site security, hot-work controls, and soft-cost exposure.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Project value above $50M
- Renovation of an occupied high-rise
- Delay-in-completion above 12 months

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
