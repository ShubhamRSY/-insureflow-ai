## 1. Product overview

Expropriation, currency inconvertibility, political violence, and related PRI covers.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Investors with cross-border assets exposed to expropriation, inconvertibility, and political violence.
- Acceptable obligor/buyer credit ratings
- Bonded principals with demonstrated capacity and collateral
- M&A transactions with vetted, audited financials

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Exposures in sanctioned countries (OFAC)
- No sovereign transfer or convertibility guarantee
- Confiscation exposure with no bilateral investment treaty
- Unrated counterparties in the host country

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Political risk application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Country / project exposure schedule | Confirms the project scope, schedule, and construction risk. |
| Investment structure and ownership docs | Confirms investment suitability, SEC/FINRA disclosure, and the securities-account setup. |
| Host contracts / concession agreements | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Currency and repatriation plan | Assesses environmental and pollution exposure and controls. |
| Security / political violence assessment | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Financial projections for insured investment | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Prior PRI policy / claims (if any) | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- War/confiscation unless endorsed
- Ordinary trade credit risk
- Currency devaluation (varies)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Currency inconvertibility
- Sabotage/terrorism
- Arbitration award default

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Investment value per country and project
- Aggregate by peril

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Country risk rating
- Investment type and tenor
- Sanctions and governmental nexus

## 12. Underwriting focus

Evaluate country risk, investment structure, host-government relations, and tenor.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Sanctioned or high-risk jurisdictions
- Any government-owned counterparty

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
