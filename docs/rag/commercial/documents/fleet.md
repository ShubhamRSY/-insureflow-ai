# Fleet Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Auto
**Document ID:** commercial/fleet
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `fleet`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Program coverage for larger commercial vehicle fleets.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Businesses operating multiple power units under DOT or state authority.
- DOT compliance file and driver-qualification files
- MVR monitoring program

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Fleets above 25 units without telematics or a driver-safety program
- MVR/CSA scores above the underwriting threshold
- Heavy-truck fleets without maintenance and DOT files
- Unresolved large-loss history

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Application form (ACORD 127 / 137 / 163 as applicable) | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |
| Vehicle schedule (VIN, year, make, model, GVW, garaging ZIP) | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Driver schedule (name, DOB, license #, state, years experience) | KYC / identity verification and anti-fraud. |
| MVRs for all listed drivers | Verifies licensing and the driving exposure/experience of the operator. |
| Loss runs — 3–5 years | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Radius of operation / usage description | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Maintenance / safety program documentation | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Prior commercial auto declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Fleet safety / telematics program summary | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Driver hiring and MVR monitoring policy | Verifies licensing and the driving exposure/experience of the operator. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Pollution from cargo unless endorsed
- Livery use without endorsement
- Intentional damage

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Hired/Non-Owned
- Pollution buy-back (truckers)
- Scheduled/unscheduled units
- Employee-driver endorsements

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- CSL $1M–$5M by fleet size
- Physical damage by schedule
- Hired/NNO $1M

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Fleet size, radius, and commodity
- Driver MVR and experience
- Loss history and preventable-accident review

## 12. Underwriting focus

Assess fleet size growth, telematics, driver hiring standards, and catastrophic loss potential.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Fleet above 25 units
- Any fatality or hazmat incident
- Radioactive handling

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
