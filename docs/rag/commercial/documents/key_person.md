# Key Person Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Workforce

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `key_person`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Life / disability on a critical individual whose loss would financially hurt the business.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Businesses insuring a key owner/executive whose death would disrupt operations.
- State-governed workers' compensation for eligible employment classes
- Captive or self-insured groups for qualifying industries
- Documented safety program for high-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No documentation of the key person's value or replacement cost
- Business with no continuity or succession plan
- Overlapping key-person arrangements exceeding the firm's demonstrated value
- Insurable interest not established

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Application form + medical questionnaire for the insured individual | Supports medical underwriting, class assignment, and rating. |
| Paramedical exam / medical records (often required for larger amounts) | Supports medical underwriting, class assignment, and rating. |
| Financial statements showing revenue / profit attributable to the key person | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Job description and valuation / justification of coverage amount | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Corporate resolution authorizing the policy purchase | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Buy-sell agreement (if policy funds a buyout) | Reviews contractual obligations, indemnities, and risk-transfer terms. |
| Loan / financing documents (if policy is collateral) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Beneficiary designation form (usually the company itself) | Verifies dependency, relationship, and the beneficiary arrangement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Suicide within two years
- Material misrepresentation

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Accelerated Death Benefit
- Waiver of Premium

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Face amount tied to the documented value of the key person
- Financial underwriting per role

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Age/health/tobacco of the key person
- Financial justification
- Product type (term/permanent)

## 12. Underwriting focus

Evaluate the individual's health, financial impact on the business, coverage justification, and corporate authorization / buy-sell structure.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Face above the supported key-person value
- Cross-purchase/buy-sell without an agreement

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
