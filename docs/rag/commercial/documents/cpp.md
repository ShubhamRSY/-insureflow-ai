# Commercial Package Policy (CPP)

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Package
**Document ID:** commercial/cpp
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `cpp`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Customizable bundle: property + liability + crime + auto and related sections.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Mid-market accounts needing property and liability combined with line-specific forms under one policy.
- Eligible occupancy combined with property and liability cover
- Building and BPP values within program limits
- Revenue and payroll within BOP/CPP thresholds

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Unsupported combinations of coverages not filed together
- Property and liability at different insured entities without a scheduled endorsement
- Risks requiring mono-line specialty treatment
- Unlisted locations beyond the schedule

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| CPP / package application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Requested coverage sections checklist | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Property SOV / COPE | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| GL operations and sales | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Crime controls questionnaire (if included) | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Auto / fleet schedules (if included) | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Loss runs for all included lines | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Prior package declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Property and CGL exclusions apply line by line
- Professional liability (separate)
- Auto and WC (separate forms)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Hired/Non-Owned Auto
- Business Income
- Equipment Breakdown
- Employee Dishonesty
- Spoilage

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Structured by coverage; higher than BOP
- CGL $1M/$2M with per-line sub-limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Loss cost × exposure by coverage
- Package credit
- Territory and protection class

## 12. Underwriting focus

Coordinate multi-section exposures, shared limits, and package eligibility vs. monoline.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Values above CPP program capacity
- Any manuscript coverage
- Mixed habitational exposure

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
