# Crime Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Property
**Document ID:** commercial/crime
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `crime`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Employee dishonesty, forgery, burglary/robbery, and computer/funds-transfer fraud.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Businesses exposing money, securities, and other property to employee dishonesty and fraud.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No segregation of duties or dual controls over funds
- Unsigned or unverified employee background checks
- Prior fidelity loss attributable to weak internal controls
- Paper-based payments with no reconciliations

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Internal control procedures document | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Employee background check policy | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Cash handling procedures | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Loss history related to theft/fraud | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Crime / fidelity application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Prior crime policy declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Employee count and roles with financial authority | Documents the benefit-plan design, participation, and funding structure. |
| Audit / CPA management letter (if any) | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Indirect or consequential loss
- Legal fees and fines
- War and nuclear perils
- Loss discovered beyond the policy's discovery period

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Computer Fraud
- Funds Transfer Fraud
- Employee Benefit Plan (ERISA) coverage
- Client/third-party property

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Employee dishonesty per-employee and aggregate limits
- Forgery or alteration limit
- Computer/funds-transfer fraud sub-limits
- ERISA fidelity bond limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Number of employees and revenue
- Internal controls and segregation of duties
- Prior fidelity losses

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review internal controls, dual authorization, inventory/cash handling, and prior fidelity losses.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Employee dishonesty limits above $1M
- Any prior unrecovered dishonesty loss

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
