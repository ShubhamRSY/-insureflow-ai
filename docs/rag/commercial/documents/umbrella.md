# Umbrella / Excess Liability Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Liability
**Document ID:** commercial/umbrella
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `umbrella`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Excess limits over primary GL, auto, and employer liability schedules.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Insureds needing excess limits over scheduled primary policies (auto, GL, employers' liability).
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No underlying primary policies in force at the required limits
- Underlying classes excluded by the primary program
- Gaps between the underlying coverages and the umbrella attachment
- Risks for which the required underlying cover cannot be purchased

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Every exposure not covered by the scheduled underlying policies (unless self-maintained)
- The retention of the underlying
- War and nuclear perils
- Uninsurable fines and penalties

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Self-maintained/non-owned underlying
- Extended reporting for claims-made underlying
- Follow-form (or broader) wording

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Occurrence and aggregate on top of underlying
- Self-insured retention where no underlying exists
- Drop-down where the underlying is exhausted

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Underlying limits and coverage breadth
- Attachment point vs the underlying
- Catastrophe and class exposure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Verify underlying limits/schedules, drop-down triggers, and high-severity exposure classes.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Umbrella limits above $5M
- Unresolved gaps between underlying forms
- Non-standard self-maintained underlying

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
