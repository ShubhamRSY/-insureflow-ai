# Inland Marine Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Property
**Document ID:** commercial/inland_marine
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `inland_marine`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Mobile property, equipment, installation, transit, and specialty floaters.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Property in transit, mobile equipment, and floating/unscheduled property used in business.
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Cargo without a bill of lading or shipping documentation
- Overland transit through territories with active conflict
- Project cargo above $5M without an engineering stowage plan
- Recurring losses attributed to inadequate packing

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Equipment schedule with serial numbers and values | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Purchase invoices/receipts | Documents the value, age, and condition of the insured property/equipment. |
| Usage/location patterns (where equipment travels) | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Loss run reports specific to equipment theft/damage | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Inland marine application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Storage / security / GPS tracking details | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Prior inland marine declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Appraisals for scheduled specialty items | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Inherent vice and wear
- Unexplained or mysterious disappearance unless perils are specified
- Voluntary parting with property
- Employee dishonesty (crime form)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Motor Truck Cargo
- EDP / Computer Equipment
- Scheduled Property Floater
- Installation / Builder's Equipment

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Per-conveyance and per-location limits
- Scheduled vs blanket item limits
- Deductibles by class of property

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Property class and mode of transit
- Territory and hauling radius
- Value at risk and catastrophe exposure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review scheduled values, mobility/theft controls, transit routes, and installation exposure windows.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Project cargo above $5M
- Annual transit programs above $2M
- Unusual commodities (livestock, fine art, explosives)

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
