# Construction / Contractor's Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Specialty
**Document ID:** commercial/construction
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `construction`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Contractor's GL and wrap-up (OCIP/CCIP) construction programs.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** General contractors and construction managers, including wrap-up projects.
- Operators holding class-specific certification and licensing
- Documented risk-management programs and contingency plans

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Contractors without licensed or bonded status
- Wrap-up or captive projects without an engineering report
- Design-defect or legacy claims from prior projects
- Projects in distressed or delay-prone jurisdictions

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Contractor's application / construction supplemental | Confirms the project scope, schedule, and construction risk. |
| Trade mix and % subcontracted | Confirms subcontractor insurance and risk-transfer via certificates of insurance (COI). |
| Project list (values, duration, location) | Confirms the project scope, schedule, and construction risk. |
| Safety manual and EMR / OSHA logs | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |
| Subcontractor insurance requirements | Confirms subcontractor insurance and risk-transfer via certificates of insurance (COI). |
| Wrap-up (OCIP/CCIP) structure docs if applicable | Confirms the project scope, schedule, and construction risk. |
| Loss runs — GL / WC / auto | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Prior contractor / wrap declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 6. Applications & forms

- ACORD 125/126
- Construction supplemental
- Wrap-up application

## 7. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Contractor's General Liability | Trade mix and subcontracted cost percentage; Project list with values and locations |
| Wrap-Up (OCIP/CCIP) Policies | Wrap-up structure / enrolled parties schedule; Safety program and EMR documentation |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Professional/design liability (separate cover)
- Pollution unless endorsed
- Damage to the insured's own work
- Residential construction beyond program limits

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Additional insured (owners/contractors)
- Waiver of subrogation
- Completed-operations extension
- Wrap-up (OCIP/CCIP)

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- $1M/$2M per project; higher for wrap-ups
- Per-project aggregates for wrap-ups

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Type of work and subcontractor percentage
- Revenue and payroll
- Safety and loss history
- Project size and height

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review trade mix, subcontracted %, height/depth work, wrap structure, and project safety.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Residential construction above program limits
- Any wrap-up above $50M
- Defect/latent-injury history

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
