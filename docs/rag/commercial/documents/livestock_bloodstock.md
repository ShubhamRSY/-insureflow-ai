# Livestock / Bloodstock Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Specialty
**Document ID:** commercial/livestock_bloodstock
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `livestock_bloodstock`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Mortality and related covers for livestock herds and bloodstock (horses, etc.).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Owners of livestock and bloodstock (horses, cattle) with mortality exposure.
- Operators holding class-specific certification and licensing
- Documented risk-management programs and contingency plans

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Animals without veterinary or disease-free certification
- Herd located in a quarantined area
- No mortality records or agreed valuation basis
- Bloodstock without pedigree or training data

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Livestock / bloodstock application | Verifies animal identity, health, and value. |
| Veterinary records for insured animals | Verifies animal identity, health, and value. |
| Herd / animal valuation schedule | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Animal identification (tags, microchips, registration papers) | Verifies animal identity, health, and value. |
| Mortality / loss history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Biosecurity and husbandry practices | Verifies animal identity, health, and value. |
| Purchase invoices or appraisal for high-value animals | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Prior livestock / bloodstock declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 6. Applications & forms

- Livestock / bloodstock application

## 7. Coverage schedule

| Coverage / option | Supporting information |
|---|---|
| Livestock Mortality | Herd inventory with ages, sexes, and values |
| Bloodstock | Registration papers and veterinary certificate of health |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Theft without a police report
- Intentional slaughter
- Conditions not disclosed at inception

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Prospective foal
- Surgical/procedural extension
- Herd cover

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Agreed value per animal
- Herd aggregate limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Species, breed, age, and value
- Health/veterinary report
- Use (racing, breeding, commercial)

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review veterinary records, herd/animal valuation, mortality history, and biosecurity practices.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- High-value bloodstock above $1M
- Pre-existing/performance-affecting conditions

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
