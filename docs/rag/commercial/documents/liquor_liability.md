# Liquor Liability Insurance

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `liquor`

## 1. Product overview

Liability arising from selling, serving, or furnishing alcoholic beverages.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Bars, taverns, restaurants, and venues serving or furnishing alcohol.
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Premises with prior alcohol-related injury claims
- No server-training or age-verification procedures
- Late-night venues with drink-special promotions
- Prior license suspensions or dram-shop violations

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Liquor license copy | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Alcohol sales as % of total revenue | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Server training certification (e.g., TIPS/ServSafe) | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Incident/claims history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Liquor liability application / supplemental | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Hours of operation and entertainment description | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Security / ID-check procedures | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Prior liquor liability declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Injury to the insured/employees in the course of employment
- Serving visibly intoxicated persons where prohibited by statute
- Assault and battery unless endorsed
- Statutory dram-shop penalties

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Assault and Battery
- Additional insured (landlord)
- Defence costs

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Per common-cause and aggregate
- Assault-and-battery buy-back sub-limit

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Receipts, hours, and establishment type
- Training/certification (e.g., TIPS)
- Assault-and-battery history
- Prior liquor claims

## 12. Underwriting focus

Assess alcohol sales mix, training (TIPS), hours, prior dram-shop claims, and security.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Late-night/high-volume venues
- Any assault-and-battery history
- BYOB or unlicensed service

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
