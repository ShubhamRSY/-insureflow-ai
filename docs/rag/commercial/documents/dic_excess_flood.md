# Difference in Conditions (DIC) / Excess Flood

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Property
**Document ID:** commercial/dic_excess_flood
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `dic_excess_flood`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Fills gaps between primary property and named-peril exclusions (flood, quake, and other DIC perils).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Insureds filling gaps between a primary property program and an uninsured peril (flood, quake).
- ISO construction classes 1–6 (frame through fire-resistive) in good repair
- Protected risks within 1,000 ft of a hydrant and 5 road-miles of a responding fire station
- Owner-occupied and investment (tenant) occupancies supported by a current valuation
- Habitational risks only where mitigated and outside designated CAT zones

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Underlying primary flood policy not in force
- Attachment below the underlying flood limit
- Property in a special flood hazard area without primary flood cover
- Requested DIC limit beyond qualified aggregate capacity

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| DIC / excess flood application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Primary property policy declarations and exclusion schedule | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Flood zone / elevation certificate (for excess flood) | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| Earthquake / seismic exposure notes (if quake DIC) | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| SOV with TIV by location and peril | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Prior flood / quake / DIC loss history | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Underlying deductibles and sublimits schedule | Documents the primary/underlying program, limits, and attachment point. |
| Prior DIC / excess flood declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Every peril already covered by the primary property policy (DIC responds only where the primary does not)
- War and nuclear perils
- Wear, tear, and gradual deterioration
- Loss within the primary deductible

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Flood buy-back
- Earthquake buy-back
- Difference in deductibles

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- DIC attachment above the primary limit
- Flood and earthquake sub-limits
- Combined single limit over the primary

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Structure of the primary program and the gaps to fill
- CAT-zone exposure
- Retention/deductible gaps

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review primary policy exclusions specifically; structure excess flood/quake attachments and DIC peril scope.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any new CAT-zone schedule
- Attachment/deductible gaps not articulated by the broker

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
