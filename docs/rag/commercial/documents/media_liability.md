# Media Liability Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Liability

**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `media`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Defamation, privacy, IP, and content-related liability for media/publishers.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Media, publishers, broadcasters, and content creators exposing defamation and content liability.
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Content with known defamatory or infringing material
- Firms with a pattern of large media claims
- Prohibited content categories (adult, hate, gambling)
- No content-review or rights-clearance procedure

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Content description (publishing, broadcast, digital media) | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Editorial review/fact-checking process documentation | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Prior defamation/IP infringement claims | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Media liability application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Revenue by content type | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Sample contracts with freelancers / talent | Reviews contractual obligations, indemnities, and risk-transfer terms. |
| Take-down / retraction policy | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Prior media liability declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Knowingly false or malicious content
- Contractual obligations
- Prior acts before the retro date
- Failure to perform professional services
- Fines and penalties

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Prior acts extension
- Extended Reporting Period
- Advertising/online content

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Claims-made per-claim/aggregate
- Defence inside the limit
- Advertising-injury and content sub-limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Media type, reach, and content
- Prior libel/defamation claims
- Retro date and jurisdictions

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review content types, clearance procedures, prior IP/defamation claims, and distribution reach.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Investigative journalism exposure
- Any prior defamation claim

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
