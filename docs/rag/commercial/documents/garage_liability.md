# Garage Liability Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Auto
**Document ID:** commercial/garage_liability
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `garage`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Auto dealer / repair garage liability and garagekeepers coverage.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Auto dealers, service/repair shops, and parking operations.
- Private-passenger-to-commercial (classes 3–6), light/medium trucks, and tractors
- Drivers with valid CDL/MVR within program thresholds
- Garaged in an eligible state

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Dealerships with unresolved title or odometer issues
- Service or repair shops without customer-vehicle key control
- Sales lots in flood-prone or non-conforming zones
- No bailee-custody guidelines

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Garage liability application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Operations mix (sales, repair, body, storage) | Assesses environmental and pollution exposure and controls. |
| Average / max customer vehicle values on lot | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Lot security and keys control | Confirms safety, maintenance, and loss-prevention/risk-management programs. |
| Technician certifications | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Loss runs — garage / GL / auto | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Dealer plate / inventory notes | Establishes the subject-matter identity and road-legality (motor) and the operating radius. |
| Prior garage declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Customer property beyond the garagekeeper's legal liability (separate forms)
- Defective workmanship
- Racing

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Garagekeepers
- Dealers' drive-away
- Loaner/rental vehicles
- Auto inventory physical damage

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- CSL by garagekeeper exposure
- Garagekeepers legal liability per customer vehicle

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Type of operation and sales volume
- Number of service bays/loaned vehicles
- Loss history

## 12. Underwriting focus

Assess repair vs. dealer operations, lot security, customer vehicle values, and tech training.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Any fraud/misrepresentation history
- High-value exotic inventory

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
