# Architects & Engineers (A&E) Professional Liability

**Effective:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Product overview

Design professional liability distinct from general E&O — architects, engineers, and related design firms.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Design professionals — architects, engineers, and design-build firms.
- Standard ISO CGL classes with under 30% subcontracted revenue
- Products-completed-operations exposure under $10M annual receipts
- Residential construction not exceeding three stories
- Confirmed loss-control program for higher-hazard classes

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Firms doing structural work without certified engineers of record
- Undisclosed prior design litigation
- Design-build projects outside the firm's demonstrated competence
- Firms without peer-review or QA procedures

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| A&E professional liability application | Standard application; establishes the risk, requested cover, and applicant disclosures. |
| Design contracts / professional services agreements | Confirms the project scope, schedule, and construction risk. |
| Project portfolio (types, values, geographies, 3–5 years) | Confirms the notified crop/area or the parametric weather-index basis. |
| Peer review / QA/QC process documentation | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Professional licenses and firm registrations | Confirms legal capacity, licensing, ownership, and authority to bind. |
| Loss runs — design professional claims | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Revenue by discipline (architecture, civil, structural, MEP, etc.) | Confirms the event/travel exposure, authority, and itinerary. |
| Prior A&E / professional liability declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Professional liability assumed by contract beyond the standard of care
- Insured vs insured
- Fee refunds
- Joint ventures unless endorsed
- Prior acts before the retroactive date

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Design-build professional
- Joint venture
- Extended Reporting Period
- Retroactive date change

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Claims-made per-claim/aggregate
- PL/MPL structure
- Defence inside the limit

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Fee income and discipline (structural, geotechnical, etc.)
- Project type and size
- Retro date and claims
- Subconsultant exposure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Review design contracts, project portfolio hazard mix, peer review processes, and prior design-defect claims.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Tunnel/bridge/marine structural work
- Any condo or defect claim
- Fee income above $5M

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
