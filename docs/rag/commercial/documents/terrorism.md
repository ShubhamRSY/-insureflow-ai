# Terrorism Insurance

**Line of business:** Commercial Insurance (P&C) › Specialty
**Document ID:** commercial/terrorism
**Guide:** Commercial Lines Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `terrorism`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Certified and/or non-certified terrorism property and liability covers.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Property/BI exposures in TRIA-eligible or high-target locations.
- Operators holding class-specific certification and licensing
- Documented risk-management programs and contingency plans

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Government-pool eligible risks (TRIA) not carved out
- Assets clustered in a single high-profile location
- Conventional war or cyber-terror exposures
- NBCR (nuclear, biological, chemical, radiological) exposures

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Completed ACORD application(s) relevant to the line(s) requested
- Financial statements — last 2–3 years (P&L, balance sheet)
- Loss run reports from prior carrier(s) — typically 3–5 years
- Business license / entity formation documents
- Organizational chart
- Description of business operations

## 5. Submission requirements

| Document | Why it is required |
|---|---|
| Terrorism coverage application / election form | Acknowledges product disclosures and elected policy options. |
| Location schedule with TIV | Validates the sum insured, valuation basis, and limit adequacy. |
| Occupancy and public access description | Describes the operations, products, and exposures being underwritten. |
| Security measures at key locations | Assesses control maturity and cyber/security exposure quality. |
| Existing property / liability declarations | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |
| Prior terrorism losses (if any) | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Broker modeling / aggregation notes | Confirms the catastrophe zone, siting, and mitigation for rating. |
| Financial statements | Supports valuation, financial underwriting, and premium-affordability assessment. |

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Nuclear/radiological/biological/chemical unless the free-standing cover responds
- War (non-certified acts)

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- TRIA certified / non-certified
- Standalone terrorism over a property program

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Per-occurrence and annual aggregate
- Schedule-wide catastrophe limits

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Target attractiveness and occupancy
- Location and concentration
- TRIA program structure

## 12. Underwriting focus

Assess location attractiveness, aggregation, and TRIPRA vs. stand-alone needs.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Iconic/trophy-property schedules
- Aggregate exposure above program capacity

- **Authority:** Field authority to $250K premium / $5M TIV; above refers to the Commercial Desk. Any decline requires a written reason on the file.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO commercial lines forms, ACORD applications, NCCI (workers comp), AAIS (inland marine)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
