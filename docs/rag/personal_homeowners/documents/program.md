# Personal Homeowners Underwriting Program

**Line of business:** Personal Homeowners
**Document ID:** personal_homeowners/program
**Guide:** Homeowners Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Program status:** filed

## 1. Program overview

Personal Homeowners program for standard eligible risks within filed program limits.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** US homeowners (HO-3/HO-5 forms) for owner-occupied dwellings.
- Owner-occupied 1–4 family dwellings
- Updates and roof condition acceptable; CLUE acceptable
- Replacement cost within program

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Uninsurable roof, knob-and-tube wiring, or unsafe conditions
- Vacant/for-sale; excessive CLUE losses

## 4. Submission requirements

| Requirement | Document types | Why it is required |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowners application | Homeowners Application; ACORD XML | Standardized application and underwriting data for the line. |
| Dwelling inspection | Dwelling Inspection; Inspection Report | Confirms condition, occupancy, and protection exactly as rated. |
| Claims history / CLUE | Home Claims History; Loss Run | Establishes prior loss frequency and severity for experience rating and acceptability. |
| Declarations / prior policy | Dec Page | Confirms cover continuity, prior terms, and any no-claim/loss-free entitlement. |

## 5. Applications & forms

- Filed program application (ACORD or carrier-specific) plus state-mandated disclosures.

## 6. Coverage schedule

_Coverages per the filed program forms (see Policy exclusions and Endorsements below)._

## 7. Policy exclusions

- Flood and earthquake (separate)
- Wear, tear, and neglect
- Ordinance/law; business and rental exposures beyond permitted

## 8. Endorsements & optional covers

- Water backup and sump overflow
- Replacement cost on contents
- Scheduled personal property
- Separate wind/hail deductible

## 9. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Coverage A/B/C/D with typical HO-3 limits
- AOP and separate wind/hail deductibles

## 10. Rating & pricing factors

- Construction, age, roof, protection class, and territory
- CLUE/MVR; replacement cost vs ACV

## 11. Underwriting focus

Confirm eligibility, condition, and rating factors for the risk per the Personal Homeowners program before binding.

## 12. Decline / refer authority

- Coastal/wind or wildfire zones
- Roof age over 20 years; prior water losses

- **Authority:** Field authority to program limits; refer coastal/wildfire zones, aged roofs, and excessive CLUE losses.

## 13. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ISO HO-3/HO-5 forms, CLUE, state DOI filed rules
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.

## 14. Underwriting file checklist

- Homeowners Application
- ACORD XML
- Dwelling Inspection
- Inspection Report
- Home Claims History
- Loss Run
- Dec Page
