# Deferred Annuity

**Line of business:** Life Insurance › Annuity

**Guide:** Life Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `deferred_annuity`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Accumulates money now for income payments that begin later.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Long-horizon retirement accumulation with tax deferral.
- Single and flexible premium within product ages
- Suitability review for all; registered representative and prospectus for variable

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Premiums or deferral period outside the product terms
- No beneficiary designation
- Qualified/non-qualified status not established
- Surrender-charge schedule not disclosed

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Social Security Number (SSN)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or lease — last 3 months)
- Completed life insurance application
- Signed HIPAA authorization (medical records release)
- Signed MIB (Medical Information Bureau) and Rx database check authorization
- Health questionnaire (part of application)
- Beneficiary's full name, DOB, SSN, and relationship to insured
- Income proof (pay stubs, W-2, or tax returns) — required above coverage thresholds
