# Level Term Life Insurance

**Line of business:** Life Insurance › Term

## 1. Product overview

Guaranteed level premium and death benefit for a fixed term. Base set only — a paramedical exam is required above ~$100K–$250K (insurer-dependent).

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Pure protection for income replacement, mortgage and debt cover, and business obligations.
- Issue ages 18–75 with standard through preferred classes
- Face amounts $100K–$5M (financial underwriting above thresholds)
- US residents; non-US citizens require a documented US nexus

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- Terminal illness or life expectancy under 12 months
- Active substance abuse without 24 months of documented sobriety
- Uncontrolled serious cardiac or oncologic condition
- Multiple declines or flat extras above $10/$1,000

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Social Security Number (SSN)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or lease — last 3 months)
- Completed life insurance application
- Signed HIPAA authorization (medical records release)
- Signed MIB (Medical Information Bureau) and Rx database check authorization
- Health questionnaire (part of application)
- Beneficiary's full name, DOB, SSN, and relationship to insured
- Income proof (pay stubs, W-2, or tax returns) — required above coverage thresholds

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Suicide within the first two policy years
- Material misrepresentation within the two-year contestable period
- War or hazardous aviation unless excluded-rated

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Accidental Death Benefit
- Waiver of Premium
- Critical Illness rider
- Child Term rider
- Disability Income rider

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Face of 10–20× income for financial underwriting
- Convertible to permanent while the term is in force
- Renewable annually to age 80–95 depending on the form

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Attained age, sex, and tobacco status
- Health class (preferred plus through rated tables)
- Face-amount band and term duration
- Avocation and motor-vehicle records

## 12. Underwriting focus

Standard medical underwriting with class assignment; verify duration choice matches the need horizon and confirm the paramedical threshold for the requested face.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Sum assured above $1M or the financial-underwriting threshold
- Rated class or flat extra above the field limit
- Attained age outside the field issue band

- **Authority:** Field authority to $1M face / Table 2; above, or any flat extra > $5/$1,000, refers to the Life Chief Underwriter.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACORD 100 series, MIB, HIPAA authorizations, NAIC model regs, IRC 7702 / 101(g)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
