# Child ULIP

**Line of business:** Life Insurance › Ulip

**Guide:** Life Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `child_ulip`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Unit-linked plan on a child with the parent / proposer paying premiums.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Market-linked cover/savings funding a child's future education or milestones.
- Registered representative and suitability review; prospectus delivered
- Issue ages 18–75 with adequate funding

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No parent-child insurable interest
- Child beyond the eligible age band
- Premium below the product minimum
- Payout structure outside the product schedule

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Social Security Number (SSN)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or lease — last 3 months)
- Completed life insurance application
- Signed HIPAA authorization (medical records release)
- Signed MIB (Medical Information Bureau) and Rx database check authorization
- Health questionnaire (part of application)
- Beneficiary's full name, DOB, SSN, and relationship to insured
- Income proof (pay stubs, W-2, or tax returns) — required above coverage thresholds

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Investment losses borne by the owner
- Suicide/contestability
- Payout restricted to the defined milestones; limited early liquidity

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Waiver of Premium (payer)
- Milestone payout rider

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- Fund-value-based payouts at milestones
- Payer waiver on death/disability

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Payer mortality and charges
- Allocation and fund fees

## 12. Underwriting focus

Base set applies to the parent / proposer (not the child); verify the child's details and premium waiver rider.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Aggressive allocations
- Short horizon vs equity exposure

- **Authority:** Field authority to $1M face / Table 2; above, or any flat extra > $5/$1,000, refers to the Life Chief Underwriter.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACORD 100 series, MIB, HIPAA authorizations, NAIC model regs, IRC 7702 / 101(g)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
