# Guaranteed Universal Life (GUL)

**Line of business:** Life Insurance › Universal
**Document ID:** life/guaranteed_universal_life
**Guide:** Life Underwriting Guide · Version 3.0
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Package checklist key:** `guaranteed_universal_life`
**Program status:** live

## 1. Product overview

Universal life with a no-lapse guarantee — premium and death benefit guaranteed to a stated age or lifetime.

## 2. Target market, appetite & eligibility

- **Market:** Flexible permanent coverage — guaranteed universal life, current-assumption, indexed (IUL), and variable (VUL).
- Issue ages 18–85 with adequate funding for the no-lapse guarantee
- Registered representative and prospectus for VUL (FINRA/SEC)

## 3. Ineligible / decline criteria

- No-lapse guarantee premium schedule that cannot be funded
- Attained age beyond the GUL issue limits
- Premium shortfall against the no-lapse schedule
- Riders outside the product's filing

## 4. Core submission packet (all products in this line)

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Social Security Number (SSN)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or lease — last 3 months)
- Completed life insurance application
- Signed HIPAA authorization (medical records release)
- Signed MIB (Medical Information Bureau) and Rx database check authorization
- Health questionnaire (part of application)
- Beneficiary's full name, DOB, SSN, and relationship to insured
- Income proof (pay stubs, W-2, or tax returns) — required above coverage thresholds

## 8. Policy exclusions

- Suicide and contestability for two years
- For VUL: market risk is borne by the owner

## 9. Endorsements & optional covers

- Accelerated Death Benefit
- Waiver of Monthly Deductions
- Rider sum increases
- Overloan protection

## 10. Limits, deductibles & retention guidance

- GUL: guaranteed death benefit with a minimum premium
- IUL caps 9–12% with participation rates and a floor
- VUL separate accounts with a delivered prospectus

## 11. Rating & pricing factors

- Health class; funding level affects lapse risk
- Interest/index assumptions and policy charges
- Rider loadings

## 12. Underwriting focus

Guaranteed premium / coverage schedule, lapse-protection mechanics, and funding discipline.

## 13. Decline / refer authority

- Requested funding below the no-lapse schedule
- Rider or death-benefit-option changes
- Attained age near the GUL maximum

- **Authority:** Field authority to $1M face / Table 2; above, or any flat extra > $5/$1,000, refers to the Life Chief Underwriter.

## 14. Regulatory & filing notes

- **Standards:** ACORD 100 series, MIB, HIPAA authorizations, NAIC model regs, IRC 7702 / 101(g)
- Rates, rules, and forms are state-filed. Confirm the issue state's mandated benefits, rating restrictions, and any surplus-lines or stamping requirements before binding.
