## High finding

**H1 — A second location stated only in words is not detected. The out-of-scope file reaches "review pending" with a market and the send enabled (owner @ai-engineer-assistant; decision @user).**
- What happens: SUB-475's application has one `Premises address` line. The second location appears only in prose: "Operations: Bicycle retail and repair, two stores" and "Square footage: 3,000 (Corpus), 1,800 (Rockport)". The email names it: "a repair and rental store on Austin St in Rockport". There is no schedule. The scorer records the leak as `field shown, complete status, suggested market, send enabled`. The status was "complete — review pending".
- Cause (from reading `ai/scope.py`, not executed): `multiple_locations` fires only on two distinct premises addresses, a numbered location label, a "second/additional location" line, a Locations heading, or schedule address rows. A count word ("two stores"), place-labelled square footages, and a second street named in the email match none of these. SUB-473 and SUB-474, which were detected, both have a `schedule.csv`. So did the earlier criterion-2 files that used the same prose (SUB-232, 268, 369). This is the first prose-only criterion-2 file in a hold-out, and the rule had never been tested against it.
- Why it is High: a multi-location BOP set up as a single location leaves the Rockport store unrated and possibly uncovered. The pipeline offers it to a carrier as an ordinary file. C1 still forces an acknowledgment before send, so a named underwriter must sign off. But the card gives no reason pointing to the second store. The operations text says "two stores" with no flag on it, which is exactly the one-click acknowledgment risk behind C1/C2.
- Remediation (all of these):
  1. A stricter-only scope rule under a new P-### (`approve change P-###`, D-017). It sends a file to manual intake when any in-scope document has (a) a count of places ("two/2/both/multiple stores, shops, locations, sites, branches"), (b) square footage or values labelled with two different place names, or (c) a street address or town in the email or another document that differs from the premises line and is not a mailing, prior or owner's-home address. Reason code `multiple_locations`, with the evidence quoted on the card (D-065 "What's new" line, screenshot on a temp copy).
  2. Dev self-checks on new files, never SUB-475. Positives: the three shapes above. Near-pass negatives must stay in scope: a mailing address, a prior address, "two floors / two buildings at one address", "two stores" naming a past or sold location, and a supplier's address in the email.
  3. Request equality on the existing dev set and `tests/pipeline` green. An @model-risk check with no High (one look-again max, D-129).
  4. A fresh hold-out, gold first, lint-passing, look-again on every gold fix, run once and scored once on a re-frozen commit (D-020). Size: 20 in-scope + 12 out-of-scope, with at least 6 criterion-2 files, of which at least 4 are prose-only (no schedule, one premises line). At least 3 in-scope near-pass files mention another address that is not a location. It must meet **every** gate T-120 met (fields, C1–C4, routes, conflicts 100% on ≥ 5, false manual ≤ 2/20), plus 0 leaks and 100% detection recall. The operations gate C1–C7 carries over unchanged.
