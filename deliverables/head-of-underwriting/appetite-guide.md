# Risk appetite guide — small commercial package (v1 pilot)

- Author: @head-of-underwriting
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Revised 2026-10-09 under D-003. Waiting on @senior-underwriter to confirm the edit.
- Reviewers: @senior-underwriter, @actuary, @compliance
- Assumption: US package property + GL only. Commercial auto, workers comp, and standalone umbrella are **out of appetite**, not "refer."

Profitability first. A class is in appetite only if we can explain a decline in one sentence a regulator can read.

## Size

| Gate | Target | Outside pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Account premium (package) | ≤ $25,000 | > $25,000 refer to a human; > $75,000 out of pilot |
| TIV per location | ≤ $5,000,000 | > $5,000,000 out of pilot |
| Locations | 1–3 | 4–10 refer; > 10 out of pilot |
| Revenue (GL exposure base) | ≤ $10,000,000 | > $10,000,000 out of pilot |
| Building age | Known | Unknown building age cannot auto-quote |

## Territory

- In appetite: non-coastal US.
- **Out of appetite for the pilot:** Florida; Tier 1 wind counties in TX, LA, AL, MS, SC, NC, GA; any risk written in a residual wind pool.
- Wildfire and convective-storm concentration is not banned. It is always a referral until @actuary sets a PML cap. Assumption: no PML cap exists yet.

## Target classes (eligible for the auto-quote band if every authority gate passes)

- Office: tenants and office-building lessors, no lab or medical occupancy.
- Retail: dry goods, clothing, professional services storefront. No restaurant, no liquor sales > 10% of receipts.
- Wholesale: light, no cold storage, no hazardous commodities.

No other class is a target. "Target" means eligible for auto-quote, not "we like this risk."

## Restricted (human referral only — never auto-quote)

- All habitational and lessor's risk, including 1–9 units. Senior referral.
- All contractors, including artisan with no roofing. Senior referral. Adopted @actuary's objection: planning LR does not clear the margin.
- Restaurants and any cooking occupancy, including food trucks. Senior referral.
- Vacant buildings **under renovation with a signed GC contract**. True vacancy is prohibited.
- Any account with a liability loss in 5 years, or a property loss > $25,000 in 5 years.

## Prohibited (decline only by a human, citing this list)

- Student housing and subsidized housing.
- Bars, nightclubs, hookah, adult entertainment; liquor receipts > 50%.
- Cannabis cultivation, processing, or retail.
- Fireworks, explosives, ammunition manufacturing.
- Armed security, bail bonds.
- Sawmills, pallet manufacturing, plastics manufacturing, recycling.
- Fully vacant property, and buildings with no utilities.
- Coastal wind as defined above.
- Risks where the only reason to decline is price. Price is a referral to @actuary, not a decline reason.

## Knock-out rules (IF / THEN)

1. IF class is on the prohibited list THEN decline, reason = class name, human sends the notice.
2. IF territory is out of appetite THEN decline, reason = territory exclusion.
3. IF 5-year currently valued carrier loss runs are missing, OR the only loss document is a "no known loss" letter, THEN Incomplete. Do not quote, do not decline, and do not store zero claims.
4. IF construction, protection class, or year built is unknown THEN refer; do not auto-quote.
5. IF habitational AND cooking AND no sprinklers THEN decline, reason = habitational cooking without sprinklers. Human sends the notice.
6. IF class is habitational, contractor, or restaurant THEN senior referral, never auto-quote.

## What I will not defend

- A silent auto-decline.
- An auto-quote on a restaurant, any contractor, or any habitational risk.
- Any price different from the filed rate.
- Treating a no-known-loss letter as a clean loss history.

Planning loss ratio from @actuary (not our experience): permissible 0.65; auto-quote only if the segment planning LR is ≤ 0.60. Office / dry retail / light wholesale at 0.58 passes. Artisan at 0.72 does not, and is not in the auto-quote band. Nothing here approves a bind until counsel confirms the writing entity and the rate manual (R-006).
