# Carrier match — from the shared appetite guide

- Author: @head-of-underwriting
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Accepted (T-018). Not a production submission rule.
- Source: `deliverables/head-of-underwriting/carrier-appetite.md` (copy of the guide the carriers shared).
- The earlier "no restaurant market" line was wrong. Carrier B writes restaurants.

The match is a recommendation. The underwriter picks the carrier. The system must not send a package because a rule matched, and it must not decline the producer.

## Carrier A

Offices, dry retail, light wholesale. TIV up to $5,000,000. Not a restaurant, not a contractor, not habitational.

## Carrier B

Restaurants and light contractors with 3 clean loss years. A missing loss run or a no-known-loss letter is not "3 clean years." Roofing is not light contracting.

## Carrier C

Broader appetite, higher price. Habitational only with sprinklers. Not a substitute for A or B when those fit.

## No match (not a decline)

Fireworks, explosives, cannabis, armed security, student housing, subsidized housing, bars and nightclubs. Say no placeholder match. Do not email the producer a decline.

## What I will not defend

Submitting to a carrier without the workbench decision. Treating Carrier B as if it refused restaurants.
