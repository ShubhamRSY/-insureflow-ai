Why it matters for fairness:
- An ordinary Spanish-language apartment or office file is labelled "housing program" on the card. Language is a national-origin proxy; the label puts the program frame in front of the underwriter for Spanish files only, and the office case changes the suggested market and adds a hold.
- It corrupts the monitoring that condition 4 depends on: the housing-program referral and decline rates would count Spanish bedroom-apartment files, so a language effect would read as a housing-program effect.
- The common Spanish wordings for the same programs fall to R8 (no market, overridable), while the English twin gets a senior referral. That is the wording-escape item 6 was meant to close, in one language only.

What resolves it (all three, then re-run items 1, 3 and 6 with these pairs added to `PAIRS`/`HOUSING` in the script):
1. Remove `dormitorio`, `dormitorios` from `HOUSING_PROGRAM`; add `residencia estudiantil`, `residencias estudiantiles` as the twins of dormitory/dormitories.
2. Add `vivienda de interés social` (twin of subsidized housing) and `vivienda para estudiantes` (twin of student housing).
3. Add a probe that "apartamentos de 2 dormitorios" = "2-bedroom apartments" (R12) and "oficina con dormitorio" = "office with bedroom".

Step 1's removal changes no route for the bedroom cases except the office one (stricter-neutral); steps 1–2's additions move Spanish wordings from R8 to a senior referral. Whether that is inside the approved P-088 ("plus the Spanish twins") or needs a new P-### is @user's call; I recommend treating it as a translation fix under P-088.
