# Credit Management Policy

Insurance line: trade_credit
Credit management policy document:

- New buyers: credit check via Dun & Bradstreet before limit > $25,000
- Limits reviewed quarterly
- Past-due > 60 days: shipments held pending payment plan
- Export buyers: confirmed letter of credit preferred above $100,000
- Collection escalation at 90 days to outside counsel
How credit limits are set/monitored: documented above.
