Underwriting desks

Built for line and staff underwriters

Line underwriters in the branch run the files. Staff underwriters at home office set the policy the line desk follows. Rytera covers both — and the overlap on large or unusual accounts.

Line underwriter desk

Regional and branch underwriters implement the underwriting process.

  • Producer & policyholder service — quotes, endorsements, certificates, renewals

Staff underwriter desk

Home-office underwriters make and implement underwriting policy.

  • Market research, coverage development, and rating-plan reviews (industry loss costs from ISO / AAIS / NCCI)
  • UW guides, policy statements, and branch file audits
  • Experience evaluation and technical training for line underwriters

Ratemaking & Pricing

Rates built like an actuary builds them

Ratemaking means turning past claims into future prices. Rytera uses the three textbook methods — pure premium (expected claims), loss ratio (claims vs premium), and judgment — and checks every rate is enough to pay claims, not excessive, and not unfair. It also models money set aside for claims still open, investment income on that money, and expenses split fairly across lines.

Base-rate build-up

The three-step process: future claims (pure premium) + future expenses (expense loading) = base rate, then load for contingencies and profit.

Loss ratio method

Projected loss ratio (trend × loss development) vs permissible loss ratio to compute the indicated rate change.

Statutory review

Every rate is verified adequate, not excessive, and free of unfairly discriminatory classification before it is offered.

Industry starting rates

ISO, AAIS, NCCI, and Surety Association publish loss costs — a starting price for claims. You add your own expenses and profit. We do not skip that and invent a number.
