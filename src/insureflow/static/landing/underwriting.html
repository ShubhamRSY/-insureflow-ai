Ratemaking & Pricing

Rates built like an actuary builds them

Ratemaking turns past loss statistics into future rates. Rytera prices with the three textbook methods — pure premium, loss ratio, and judgment — and checks every rate against the statutory goals of adequate, not excessive, and not unfairly discriminatory, plus the five ideal rate characteristics. It also models loss reserve estimation, investment income on reserves, and projected expenses with proper general-administrative allocation across lines.

Base-rate build-up

The three-step process: future claims (pure premium) + future expenses (expense loading) = base rate, then load for contingencies and profit.

Loss ratio method

Projected loss ratio (trend × loss development) vs permissible loss ratio to compute the indicated rate change.

Statutory review

Every rate is verified adequate, not excessive, and free of unfairly discriminatory classification before it is offered.

Advisory loss costs

ISO, AAIS, NCCI, and Surety Association loss costs — carriers add their own expense and profit factors.
