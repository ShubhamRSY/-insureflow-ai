One workbench for every submission

From a messy pile to a memo you can sign. Rytera reads, checks, and prices. You still decide. Not a stack of parsed PDFs you have to rewrite.

The problem & the fix

Built for decisions, not document storage

Old workbenches were built to hold files. Underwriters were left to read them all. Rytera flips the order: we read, check, and price. Your team still decides. Every desk word below is also in In plain English.

The old workbench
  • Files arrive by fax, email, or a shared drive — you hunt
  • You sort the queue yourself and look for red flags by hand
  • You type the same numbers into a second system
  • A yes or no only after hours of reading
  • What you will write, and how much is at risk, live in someone’s head
  • No paper trail of why a file was declined
Rytera
  • Files arrive where they already live: email, a cloud folder, or a secure drop. Other folders wait until you actually connect them
  • The queue is sorted by what you will write, how big the file is, and how much is at risk
  • Documents are read and duplicates removed. You do not re-type
  • A memo you can sign in minutes — not a pile of notes
  • What you will write, how the building is built (COPE), and how much is at risk — scored before you open the file
  • A sealed paper trail behind every decision

How the workbench runs

Triage the queue. Verify the risk. Sign the memo.

Three steps every file follows: sort the queue so the right submissions rise first, check the risk and price from your filed rates, then hand you a recommendation a licensed underwriter can sign. You work exceptions — not the pile.

The important files rise first

The live queue is ranked by what you will write, how big the file is, and how much is at risk. You see the ones that matter before the ones that don’t.

Not a generic score

Filters for what you will write, coastal catastrophe, workers-comp class codes, and loss history fire before a human opens the file.

A person stays in charge

A licensed underwriter signs. Who can sign what size of risk is written down. AI proposes. You dispose. Every change you make is recorded.

Grows with you

Practice mode first (no policy issued). Then real outside data. Then the system that issues the policy. Nothing goes live until you say so.

Each line of business has its own checks and prices from your filed rates. Pick a desk, or see the shared Connect & pull intake that meets the file where it already lives.

How we catch a wrong number

A figure that looks legal can still be impossible.

These checks actually run. We do not list science we have not built. If two facts cannot both be true, or we are not sure we read the number, a person sees it. Words of the desk.

Related facts have to agree

A driver license issued after the policy starts. Payroll of $5M with zero employees. The deductible bigger than the limit.

What Rytera does

Cross-field rules. If two numbers on the same file cannot both be true, the memo does not swallow them. You see the bruise.

Size has to match value

A 1,200 sq ft house claiming $15M to rebuild is not the same as a warehouse. $15M can be right — just not for that building.

What Rytera does

Conditional bounds. Rebuild cost is checked against square footage. A small dwelling cannot wear a commercial number quietly.

The same figure, twice

Page 1 says $500,000 incurred. Page 4 says $120,000. Someone has to notice.

What Rytera does

If two reads disagree, we do not pick the pretty one. Disagreement goes to you. Low confidence never glides into a yes.

It has to live on a page

A diagnosis, an exclusion, a total — if we cannot point at the file, it is not a fact.

What Rytera does

Every extraction cites the page. Outside claim history and driving records are used only when your accounts are connected. We never invent a clean history.

A photo can lie

A repaired roof in Photoshop is not an inspection. Messaging apps strip the camera tag. Someone has to notice.

What Rytera does

We read EXIF / ELA software tags and JPEG recompress scars (error-level analysis). Edit software or a local paste gets flagged. We ask for the original camera file. We do not claim a crime lab.

The same ring, new letterhead

A declined file comes back under a cousin’s name, same phone, same drop address.

What Rytera does

A graph net links files that share a phone, email, tax ID, address, or IP, then scores the cluster. Isolated files stay quiet. Rings do not.

The questionnaire is not the car

Stated mileage, MFA “yes” on a cyber form — easy to type, hard to live.

What Rytera does

When a connected-car or vulnerability-scan account is live, we compare the answers to the feed. Simulated never invents a clean score. Missing keys become a finding, not a green light.
