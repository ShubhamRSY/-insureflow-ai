The problem & the fix

Built for decisions, not document storage

Legacy workbenches were built to hold files. Underwriting was left to do the hard part — reading them all. Rytera flips the order: the platform does the reading, scoring, and pricing, so your team makes the decisions.

Legacy Workbench
  • Intake from a fax queue or a shared drive
  • Manual triage — underwriters hunt for red flags
  • Re-keying data across screens and systems
  • Decisions after hours of document review
  • Appetite and exposure checked in the underwriter's head
  • No audit trail of why a file was declined
Rytera AI-native
  • Intake from 24 connectors — email, S3, SharePoint, SFTP
  • Context-aware triage that prioritizes by appetite, impact, and exposure
  • Documents parsed, reconciled, and deduplicated automatically
  • Bind-ready UW memo in minutes — not hours
  • Appetite filters, COPE, and exposure scored before a human opens the file
  • Encrypted audit trail behind every decision
