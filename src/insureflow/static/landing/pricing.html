What Desk+ actually turns on

Product, not a services wrapper around fake data

Implementation is paid and real: wire live oracles, load your SERFF book, connect Guidewire. Until those three are live, Rytera stays on Pilot — it will not pretend otherwise.

1. Live oracles

Set ORACLE_MODE=live and vendor keys. Desk+ refuses simulated CLUE / NCCI / A+ / CAT. Missing keys become a critical finding, not a clean history.

2. Your rate book

POST /rating/carrier-book or set CARRIER_BOOK_PATH to your SERFF JSON/CSV. Pilot manuals cannot price a Desk+ quote.

3. Guidewire bind

Live PAS receives the full payload — limits, deductibles, filing ID, subjectivities, indicated premium. Bind is refused if the adapter is still simulated.

Plan gate

GET /billing/plan shows entitlements and whether oracles, the carrier book, and PAS are actually ready. Buyers can verify before they pay.
