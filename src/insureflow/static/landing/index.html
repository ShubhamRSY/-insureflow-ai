Trust & Governance Architecture

Grounded Risk Intelligence. Built for Regulated Trust.

Underwriters operate in high-liability, heavily audited environments where hallucinations mean catastrophic risk. Rytera is engineered around 5 uncompromising trust pillars — ensuring every decision is transparent, deterministic, auditable, and human-supervised.

Pillar 1 · Explainability

"Show Your Work" Reasoning

Verbatim Source Attribution: Every extracted figure and policy clause links directly to its original page number and bounding box.

Chain-of-Thought Auditing: Multi-agent step-by-step reasoning explains exactly why a score was assigned.

Field-Level Confidence: Individual metrics per data point — low-confidence cells route automatically to human review.

Pillar 2 · Control

Human-in-the-Loop & Overrides

Pre-Decision Review: Software synthesizes evidence and proposes — licensed underwriters retain 100% binding authority.

Frictionless 1-Click Overrides: Change any recommendation instantly with mandatory drop-down reason logging.

Active Learning Loops: Overrides feed continuous local model calibration to eliminate recurring false flags.

Pillar 3 · Guardrails

Deterministic Rule Enforcement

Hard-Coded UWGs: Compliance limits, class prohibitions, and statutory rules execute in deterministic code — never LLM guesswork.

Instant Appetite Pre-Check: Automated pre-filters screen submissions against your guidelines before deep compute begins.

State-Filed Rate Books: Pricing is derived strictly from filed actuarial manuals and SERFF schedules — zero synthetic rates.

Pillar 4 · Compliance

Immutable Audit & Reproducibility

Cryptographic Decision Logs: Timestamped SHA-256 ledger recording prompts, doc versions, agent states, and human sign-offs.

Multi-Year Reconstitution: Reconstruct the exact data state and reasoning trail for state market-conduct exams 3+ years later.

Fair Lending & Bias Auditing: Built-in monitoring dashboards track decisions to prevent algorithmic bias or disparate impact.

Pillar 5 · Uncertainty Handling

Graceful Degradation & Fail-Closed Safety

Zero Silent Failures: Blurry scans, corrupted tables, or third-party oracle timeouts cleanly halt and request human intervention rather than inventing data.

Explicit "Insufficient Data" Output: Agents are strictly trained to output "Insufficient Data" when information is missing from the submission pack — zero synthetic financial interpolation.

Zero PII Exposure: Insured names, SSNs, and tax IDs are stripped before model processing — underwriters see the real file; models see de-identified facts.
