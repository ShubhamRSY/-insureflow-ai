Plans & pricing

Priced per bind-ready memo — not per data pull

Carriers will not buy simulated oracles at Desk prices, pilot manuals sold as SERFF, or a Guidewire bind that still needs re-key. Desk+ fail-closes on all three. Shadow pilots stay free.

They will not buy if

Oracles are simulated

Fake clean CLUE / NCCI / A+ history at Desk prices is a non-starter. Desk+ requires live feeds; missing keys refer the file instead of inventing a clean loss run.

They will not buy if

Rating isn’t their book

Pilot manuals ≠ SERFF. Desk+ quotes only after you import your filed carrier book. Until then the quote is ineligible — not silently priced off our demo LCM.

They will not buy if

Bind still re-works Guidewire

Bind posts the full quote, coverages, filing ID, subjectivities, and terms. No re-key. Live PAS is required on Desk+; simulated bind is refused.
