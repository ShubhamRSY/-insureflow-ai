About Rytera

We build underwriting software for underwriters

Rytera Inc. is an AI-native underwriting platform for carriers, MGAs, and aggregators across 12 insurance sections, mortgage, and lending.

Who we build for

Life, health, general/non-life, and commercial carriers, MGAs and program administrators, mortgage lenders, and commercial lenders — one platform for every desk.

The pilot model

Shadow first, bind last. Measure accuracy on your real book before any policy admin integration turns on — with a clean cutover when you're ready.

Security & compliance
